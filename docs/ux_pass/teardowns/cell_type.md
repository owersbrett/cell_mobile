# cell_type — UX Teardown
scale: cell · duration: 60s · scoreUnit: cells
## Scores (1–5)  → TOTAL: 22/35
- Instant legibility: 3 — "WHAT KIND OF CELL?" + 4 labeled/iconed cards reads in <3s, but knowing WHICH to tap needs prior biology; the decision tree lives in GAME.md, never on screen.
- Affordance clarity: 4 — pure tap; four `_buildChoiceCard` GestureDetectors plainly read as buttons. The one ambiguity: the fact flare is tap-to-skip (`_skipFlare`) but "TAP TO CONTINUE" only appears AFTER you've already answered.
- Juice & feedback: 4 — full-stage flash ring (`flash`/`flashColor`), `FxBurst.spawn` 16-particle green burst on correct + extra gold burst every `_kStreakStep`, breathing cell, `GameFx.atmosphere` motes — all off one `_Repaint` pump → one `_StagePainter` with `RepaintBoundary`. Clean budget. Missing: no audio, no per-button squash/scale on tap.
- Fair/readable competition: 2 — purely solo. `_classify` only calls `session.addScore`/`noteStreak`; zero pass-and-play, shared-screen, or disruption layer despite the "multiplayer" rubric. The `_mult()` streak multiplier (`_kStreakStep` 3 → unbounded x) is a runaway-leader engine with no rubber-band.
- Skill depth: 3 — read-the-tells + speed + streak management is real, and `_generate` genuinely degrades tells with `_progress` (plants shed chloroplasts, bacteria rod→coccus, fungal/plant blur). But the resolving tree (nucleus→wall→chloroplast) is mechanical once learned; only 4 classes caps the ceiling.
- Pace & climax: 2 — the mandatory `_kFlareDuration` 1.9s flare gates EVERY answer (`_flareT` countdown in `_onTick` → `_loadNext`), so the round cannot accelerate; ~15–18 cells in 60s. The decay window shrinking (`_kDecayFast` 3.4→`_kDecaySlow` 1.7) is invisible to the player. No climax — last 5s just turn the timer red.
- Polish: 4 — Potatuhs font/palette, genuinely nice procedural rendering (grana stripes, mitochondrial cristae, animated flagellum, capsule halo, radial-gradient bodies). `short` layout guard for small screens. On-brand and jank-free; only the boxy plant wall looks a touch crude next to the membrane blobs.
## Top 2–3 UX failures (concrete, cite the mechanic)
1. No competitive/party surface at all. The whole loop is `session.addScore(_speedBonus() * _shownMult)` into a host-owned solo total — no pass-and-play handoff, no two-player split, no shared specimen, no "disruption facet." For a multiplayer rubric this is the headline gap, and the unbounded `_mult()` would make any naive head-to-head a runaway.
2. The 1.9s fact flare actively fights pace. `_kFlareDuration` holds for 1.9s after every single classification before `_loadNext` fires; combined with read time, the round plays at a near-constant tempo and never builds to a finish. Tap-to-skip (`_skipFlare`) exists but is undiscoverable until after the first answer, and skipping costs you the education.
3. First-timers fly blind. The four cards are weighted identically with no in-game scaffolding of the tells; the reading order that makes the game fair ("nucleus? → wall? → chloroplasts?") exists only in GAME.md. Without bio background the opening cells are pure guesses, which torpedoes instant legibility and the streak economy at the same time.
## Redesign brief — what cell_type_v2 MUST change to clear the bar
- Add a real multiplayer mode: at minimum pass-and-play (same specimen, alternating taps, fastest-correct wins the cell) or a shared-screen race; cap or rubber-band the streak multiplier so a leader can't run away. This is the gating change.
- Break the flare's grip on pace: make the fact a brief non-blocking toast (~0.6s) or fold it into the next cell's load so the round can ACCELERATE; reserve the full fact card for wrong answers / end-of-round recap. Surface the tap-to-skip hint from cell #1.
- Scaffold the read for newcomers: a first-cell coach-mark or a persistent micro-legend (icon = which tell) so the decision tree is on-screen, not in the manual; consider highlighting the deciding structure on reveal.
- Make the difficulty ramp legible and give it a climax: visibly shrink/flash the scoring window, and spike tempo + a "FINAL CELLS" beat in the last ~8s instead of only reddening the timer.
## Keep (the education + what already works — do not lose)
- The core teaching: classify a cell by its real structural tells — rigid wall, green chloroplasts (grana), large central vacuole, true nucleus vs free nucleoid, relative size, flagellum — across PLANT / ANIMAL / BACTERIAL / FUNGAL. The procedurally-drawn organelles MAP to real biology; that fidelity is the whole point and must survive.
- The resolving decision tree (nucleus present? → wall? → chloroplasts?) and the deliberate look-alike confusions (`_generate`: fungal≈plant minus chloroplasts, subtle bacteria→coccus flirting with animal) — this IS the learning curve; keep the tell-degradation by `_progress`.
- The per-answer `_kFacts` (3 per type) and the reveal-the-true-type moment on a wrong answer — strong, accurate micro-lessons; preserve the content even if the presentation timing changes.
- The clean perf architecture: single Ticker → `_Repaint` → one `_StagePainter` under a `RepaintBoundary`, with the HUD on its own `AnimatedBuilder`. Don't regress this when adding modes.
