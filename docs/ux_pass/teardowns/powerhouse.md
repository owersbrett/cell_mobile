# powerhouse — UX Teardown
scale: organelle · duration: 60s · scoreUnit: ATP
## Scores (1–5)  → TOTAL: 22/35
- Instant legibility: 3 — Two labeled feed buttons + a "TAP TO RESPIRE" pip-label read fast, but the core loop (feed BOTH tanks, THEN tap the organelle 4× while O₂ silently drains) needs the banner text to grasp; the whole-screen tap surface (`Positioned.fill` onTapDown → `_pumpTap`, line 313) means the real input target isn't where the eye thinks it is.
- Affordance clarity: 3 — All-tap, no swipe/drag ambiguity, and the `_FeedButton`s are obviously pressable. But the mitochondrion has no button-like affordance; you tap "it" yet a tap anywhere off-button fires the pump (line 316), so the tap zone is invisible and over-large.
- Juice & feedback: 4 — Strong for one-Ticker/one-Painter: per-tap squash (`_pumpKick`, line 247/531), completion glow (`_completeGlow`), sweeping progress ring (line 614), three stage pips, `FxBurst` spark count scaled by aerobic/anaerobic (16 vs 7, line 284), floating "+N ATP" pops, and an O₂-scaled readiness glow (line 539). Loses a point only for having zero audio.
- Fair/readable competition: 2 — `Role: solo high-score` (GAME.md line 12). No pass-and-play, no shared board, no opponent, no runaway-leader control, no in-run standing — it's a pure solo number via `session.addScore`. The 36-vs-2 yield gap (line 89–90) also means one player who keeps O₂ topped laps an anaerobic player ~18×, which would be a brutal runaway if ported to head-to-head as-is.
- Skill depth: 3 — Genuine tension in managing the accelerating O₂ drain (`_o2DrainStart` 0.45 → `_o2DrainEnd` 1.7, line 82–83) against feed cadence and cycle timing. But the dominant strategy is mechanical and quickly solved: top O₂ to full, ensure ≥1 glucose, spam 4 taps, repeat — and since pump taps are unrestricted spam (`_pumpPerTap` 0.25), the "drive" half has no execution challenge, only the resource half does.
- Pace & climax: 3 — The drain-acceleration curve is a real ramp that makes late-game O₂ bleed out fast (GAME.md line 84–87), so pressure builds. But there's no in-game climax beat — no final-seconds crescendo, no escalating reward, no visible countdown (host owns the clock); the arc rises then just stops when time expires.
- Polish: 4 — Clean on-brand Potatuhs palette (`inkDeep`, `gold`, accent), nicely rendered double-membrane + wavy cristae + side tanks with stoichiometry tick marks, non-finite guards throughout (lines 508, 654, 683), throttled 15fps HUD vs 60fps canvas, capped particles/pops. Minor jank: the full-screen invisible tap layer sitting under the buttons is a fragile pattern.

## Top 2–3 UX failures (concrete, cite the mechanic)
1. Solo-only with a runaway yield gap. `Role: solo high-score` and the +36/+2 aerobic-vs-anaerobic split mean there is no party/pass-and-play mode and no rubber-band — directly fails the "works pass-and-play/party, no runaway leader, legible standing" bar. Nothing on screen shows where you stand vs anyone.
2. The "tap the mitochondrion" input is a lie. `Positioned.fill` + `HitTestBehavior.opaque` routes any non-button tap to `_pumpTap` (line 313–316). New players don't know the tap target, and the organelle gets no pressed-state feedback distinct from a missed tap. The affordance the game advertises (tap the organelle) isn't the affordance it implements (tap anywhere).
3. The drive half has no skill or climax. 4 unrestricted taps complete a cycle (`_pumpPerTap` 0.25) with no rhythm, timing window, or rising stakes; combined with no final-seconds acceleration of scoring, the 60s arc plateaus instead of climaxing. The only real decision is feed-cadence, which is a solved loop within ~10s.

## Redesign brief — what powerhouse_v2 MUST change to clear the bar
- Add a real multiplayer/party shape: pass-and-play relay (each player gets a 15s shift on the same mitochondrion, ATP banked to them) or split-tank head-to-head, with a persistent on-screen score comparison so standing is legible mid-run.
- Tame the runaway: compress the aerobic/anaerobic spread (e.g. 12 vs 4, not 36 vs 2) or add catch-up (trailing player's mitochondrion runs hotter / drains slower) so one good O₂ streak doesn't end the contest.
- Make the tap target honest and skillful: constrain the pump tap to the organelle bounds (geometry is already cached, `_mitoCenter`/`_mitoRy`, line 297) and give the "drive" a timing element — e.g. a sweet-spot window on the progress ring that bonuses ATP — so the four taps reward skill, not spam.
- Build a climax: in the final ~10s, accelerate scoring (double-ATP "overdrive") or visibly spike the O₂ drain with a countdown pulse so the arc crescendos instead of flatlining.
- Add audio: a feed thunk, a pump tick, and an aerobic-vs-anaerobic completion chime — the visual juice is already strong and is carrying the whole feedback load alone.

## Keep (the education + what already works — do not lose)
- The lesson is IN the mechanic and must survive: glucose + O₂ → ATP through GLYCOLYSIS → KREBS → ELECTRON TRANSPORT, with **oxygen as the efficiency lever** (full O₂ = aerobic ~36 ATP, no O₂ = fermentation ~2 ATP). The scoring rule (`_completeCycle`, line 269–272: yield linear in O₂ used) is the teaching — do not flatten it even when compressing the spread.
- The real stoichiometry encoded in tank sizes (6 O₂ : 1 glucose, `_oxygenCap`/`_o2PerCycle` = 6 vs `_glucoseCap` discrete units) and the three named stages shown as pips — keep both; they make the abstraction faithful.
- The structural/visual teaching: double membrane, cristae folds hosting electron transport, ATP framed as rechargeable currency, and the potato-cell respiration angle (fact list line 25–35, GAME.md potato angle). The rotating fact banner is cheap and on-message — keep it.
- The perf discipline (one Ticker → one CustomPainter, no per-frame setState, throttled HUD, capped FX, non-finite guards) and the clean `_resetRun` session re-entry (the S) — keep intact.
