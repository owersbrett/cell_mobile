# osmosis — UX Teardown
scale: cell · duration: 60s · scoreUnit: pts
## Scores (1–5)  → TOTAL: 19/35
- Instant legibility: 2 — three separate readouts (top TONICITY meter, right CELL SIZE gauge, bottom slider) plus a state caption; the core loop is a two-stage indirection (you steer tonicity `_tonicity = _drift + _inject`, which integrates into volume via `dVolume/dt = -_kFlux*T`), not graspable in <3s without reading the labels.
- Affordance clarity: 2 — it's a whole-screen horizontal slider (`onTapDown`/`onPanUpdate` → `_setPointer`), but the only drawn control is a tiny ~9px handle at `y = size.height - 46` in `_paintSlider`; nothing says "the whole screen is the slider," so it reads as "drag that little knob" — the exact Farm Panic trap. Worse, a single tap on the left edge instantly slams `_inject` toward -1 (target `(_pointerX-0.5)*2`), an unintuitive jump.
- Juice & feedback: 4 — genuinely strong within budget: flux arrows across the membrane (`_paintFluxArrows`), membrane crenation/swell deformation (`crenAmp`/`swellJitter`), heal/burst full-screen flashes, `_shake`, FxBurst particles, FxPop score chips, and named event callouts — all on one Ticker → one CustomPainter. `shouldRepaint => true` plus up-to-86 solute dots is fine.
- Fair/readable competition: 2 — `Role: solo high-score` only; no pass-and-play, no party/standings, no multiplayer awareness beyond `session.addScore`. Scoring is uncapped accretive (+6/sec healthy + +35 per recovery) so a skilled player runs away while a confused one scores near zero — wide, non-legible spread for a party context.
- Skill depth: 3 — real ceiling exists: you must anticipate the accelerating drift, briefly over-correct tonicity to push volume back, and the +35 recovery bonus invites deliberate dip-and-recover gaming. But the lag between input and effect (volume integrates tonicity) makes the depth feel murky rather than masterable.
- Pace & climax: 3 — the `_progress()` ramp does accelerate honestly (drift range `0.35→0.90`, re-roll `2.4s→0.7s`, ease `1.4→4.0`), so late-game pressure is real. But there's no climax beat — the round just ends when the host clock expires; no final-seconds flourish, sting, or escalation cue inside the game.
- Polish: 3 — clean aqua/amber/green sci-bio palette, no visible jank, single-painter perf is tidy. But it carries zero Potatuhs identity (no potato, no brand type/voice); it looks like a stock biology applet, not an HPG title.
## Top 2–3 UX failures (concrete, cite the mechanic)
1. Hidden affordance: the whole play area is the slider, but the UI only draws a tiny handle at the bottom (`_paintSlider`, radius 9). Players will try to grab the knob and miss the fact that tapping/dragging anywhere works — and a tap jumps `_inject` to an extreme. No "drag here" hint, no on-touch ripple at the finger.
2. Two-stage indirection kills legibility: you control TONICITY (`_inject`) but win on VOLUME (`_volume`), and the two are coupled through an integrator with a `_kFlux = 0.20` lag plus an autonomous `_drift` you don't control. New players can't form the cause→effect map in 3 seconds; the top meter and right gauge are two different abstractions to track simultaneously.
3. Solo-only, runaway scoring: no party/pass-and-play surface and uncapped additive scoring (`_kHealthyPointsPerSec` + `_kRecoveryBonus`) means standings between players aren't close or readable — fails the fun-multiplayer bar this rubric grades on.
## Redesign brief — what osmosis_v2 MUST change to clear the bar
- Make the control obvious: render an explicit full-width track the player visibly "is," add a first-touch ripple/handle-jump-to-finger affordance, and consider relative drag (delta-based) instead of absolute jump so an edge tap doesn't slam to ±1. Add a one-frame onboarding ghost (a hand sliding L↔R) on round start.
- Collapse the indirection for first read: visually fuse the tonicity readout INTO the cell/environment (e.g. tint + flux arrows already do this) and de-emphasize one of the two HUD bars, so the player tracks ONE thing — keep the cell-size band as the single "are you safe?" signal.
- Add a multiplayer/party frame: a compact live standings strip or pass-and-play rotation, and cap/curve the score so leads stay legible (e.g. diminishing returns on long healthy streaks, or a per-round normalized band-time %).
- Add a climax: final ~10s should escalate visibly (faster/louder drift cue, color shift, countdown sting) and end on a result flourish rather than a silent clock expiry.
- Give it Potatuhs skin: the cell-in-salt-water demo IS a potato slice — lean the visual into a potato cell (turgor = firm fry vs. limp fry) so it reads as an HPG title, not a stock applet.
## Keep (the education + what already works — do not lose)
- The lesson is taught IN the mechanic and must survive: osmosis = water moves toward higher solute; the hypotonic / isotonic / hypertonic trichotomy (top meter, named live); turgor as the safe band; and lysis (burst) vs. crenation (shrivel) named on screen exactly as they happen (`_paintStateLabel`, `_failEvent` labels). The directional sub-caption ("water leaving cell →") is excellent — keep it.
- The membrane that physically crenates when shriveled and quivers taut near burst is the best teaching juice in the file — keep that deformation tied to volume.
- The accelerating `progress` difficulty ramp and the fail-and-recover-not-game-over design (cell re-forms at 0.50) are solid; preserve both.
