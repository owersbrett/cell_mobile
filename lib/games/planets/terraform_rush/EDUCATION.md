# EDUCATION.md — Terraform Rush (planets)

> The educational blocks this game engages, and HOW each is taught through the
> mechanic itself (repeated fast interaction, never text walls). A one-line
> real fact surfaces on each mission result.

- **Scale (cell):** planets
- **Game:** `terraform_rush`

## Blocks

| Block | Title | How the game teaches it | Engaged by | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| orbital_mechanics | Orbital insertion & capture | The signature mechanic IS the lesson: a dead-on shot crashes (all-radial velocity), too shallow escapes (past capture speed), and the sweet spot — mostly tangential, below the ceiling — spirals in. Players FEEL radial vs tangential velocity within three launches. | v1 mechanic | ✅ |
| gravity_assist | Gravity bends trajectories | The live preview shows the planet's gravity curving every aim in real time; power and angle trade off visibly. | v1 mechanic | ✅ |
| water_delivery | Where oceans come from | Desert worlds are solved with an Ice Asteroid — mirroring the leading theory that comets/asteroids delivered much of Earth's water. Fact line on success. | Ice Asteroid | ✅ |
| greenhouse_effect | Atmospheres trap heat | Dead Rock (no atmosphere rim) must get an Atmosphere Generator before anything can live; the rim then visibly appears. Fact line names the greenhouse effect. | Atmosphere Generator | ✅ |
| habitable_zone | Liquid water = life's precondition | The archetype ladder gates life on air + water; Colony Pods only work on already-living worlds. | Colony Pod / ladder order | ✅ |
| evolution_origin | Life starts microbial | Barren worlds take a Fungus Pod, not settlers — microbes ran Earth alone for ~3 billion years before anything else. | Fungus Pod | ✅ |
| ecological_succession | Succession builds ecosystems | The v1 seven archetypes ARE a succession ladder: air → microbes → water → plants → settlers. Forests only take on Grassland (living soil), never bare rock. | Forest Capsule / ladder | ✅ |
| planetary_engineering | Terraforming & its ethics | The whole game is planetary engineering; the Thriving World teaches its first ethic — a healthy biosphere needs NO intervention (WAIT is the answer). | WAIT / Thriving World | ✅ |
| civilization_filters | Civilizations can end themselves | The War Planet needs a Peace Beacon, not another Colony Pod — the trap decoy teaches that more people don't fix a conflict. | Peace Beacon | ✅ |
| moon_stabilization | Moons stabilize axial tilt | Ancient Planet → Moon slingshot (spec'd, v2). | v2 | ⚠️ |
| water_cycle | Ocean/continent balance | Ocean World → Tectonic Charge; plate tectonics raise land (spec'd, v2). | v2 | ⚠️ |
| pollution_repair | Ecology can be repaired | Polluted Planet → Nanobot Cloud path-trace (spec'd, v2). | v2 | ⚠️ |

## Fact lines (shown one per mission result, in-context)

- Ice Asteroid: "Earth's oceans likely arrived on icy asteroids and comets."
- Atmosphere Generator: "An atmosphere traps heat — the greenhouse effect keeps worlds warm."
- Fungus Pod: "Microbes ran Earth alone for about 3 billion years."
- Forest Capsule: "Forests build soil and pump oxygen — succession in fast-forward."
- Colony Pod: "In the habitable zone, water stays liquid — and life can settle."
- Peace Beacon: "A civilization's greatest filter can be itself."
- WAIT: "A thriving biosphere needs no engineering. Leave it be."
- Crash fail: "Too steep — orbital insertion needs sideways speed, not a dive."
- Aether fail: "Too shallow — past escape velocity, gravity can't hold on."

## Association verdict

KEEP — every v1 payload/archetype pair maps to a real planetary-science block
and is engaged mechanically, not cosmetically. v2 blocks are spec'd in GAME.md.
