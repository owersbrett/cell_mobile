# EDUCATION.md — Cosmic Timeline v2

> The E in GAMES. The education is IN the mechanic: you cannot score without
> judging where each epoch falls in cosmic history and reading the real moment it
> happened. v2 keeps every bit of the v1 lesson and adds *more* of it — a larger
> catalog and relative-ordering decisions instead of one memorized list.

## Learning objective
By playing, the student internalizes:
1. **The sequence of cosmic history** — the named epochs from the Big Bang to
   now, in order, including finer ones (Planck, Reionization, Cosmic Noon) and
   Earth-history beats (Cambrian, dinosaurs, the impact, humans).
2. **The real "when"** — the timestamp each epoch reveals when placed correctly
   (3 minutes vs 380,000 years vs 13.8 billion years).
3. **Relative ordering as reasoning, not recall** — because only a moving window
   of ~5 epochs is on screen and the catalog is large, you must reason "does this
   come before or after the cards I can see," not regurgitate a fixed 1–11 list.
4. **Deep-time intuition** — the logarithmic ribbon plots every locked epoch by
   `log10(time-since-Big-Bang)`: the universe's first second holds as much
   structure as the billions of years since; early epochs spread out, recent eons
   cram together.
5. **Causal ordering** — atoms can't precede nuclei, stars can't precede atoms,
   galaxies can't precede stars, the Sun can't precede galaxies. Order is
   causation.

## The epochs (chronological, all 18)
| # | Epoch | When (after Big Bang) | What happens |
|---|-------|------------------------|--------------|
| 1 | Big Bang | t = 0 | Space, time and energy erupt from a hot, dense state. |
| 2 | Planck Epoch | 10⁻⁴³ s | Gravity splits from the other forces — the earliest describable instant. |
| 3 | Inflation | 10⁻³² s | The universe doubles in size dozens of times in a flash. |
| 4 | Quark Soup | 10⁻⁶ s | Too hot for protons — a plasma of free quarks and gluons. |
| 5 | First Nuclei | 3 min | Protons + neutrons fuse into the first hydrogen and helium nuclei. |
| 6 | CMB / Recombination | 380,000 yrs | Atoms form; light breaks free as the cosmic microwave background. |
| 7 | Dark Ages | ~100 Myr | No stars yet — only cooling hydrogen gas in the dark. |
| 8 | First Stars | ~200 Myr | Gravity ignites the first stars, flooding the cosmos with light. |
| 9 | First Galaxies | ~400 Myr | Stars gather by the billions into the first galaxies. |
| 10 | Reionization | ~1 Gyr | Starlight re-ionizes the hydrogen fog; the universe turns transparent. |
| 11 | Cosmic Noon | ~3.3 Gyr | Peak star formation across the galaxies. |
| 12 | Our Sun Forms | ~9.2 Gyr | A cloud collapses into the Sun and its planets, including Earth. |
| 13 | Life on Earth | ~10 Gyr | The first single-celled life appears in Earth's young oceans. |
| 14 | Cambrian Explosion | ~13.2 Gyr | The seas fill with complex animals. |
| 15 | Dinosaurs Reign | ~13.6 Gyr | Dinosaurs rule the land for 150+ million years. |
| 16 | Dinosaurs End | ~13.73 Gyr | An asteroid ends the dinosaurs; mammals inherit the Earth. |
| 17 | First Humans | ~13.8 Gyr | Modern humans appear — a blink ago on the cosmic clock. |
| 18 | Now | 13.8 Gyr | You, reading this — 13.8 billion years after it all began. |

## How the mechanic teaches
- **Tap-the-gap = an ordering judgment** — every card forces "before or after the
  visible epochs?", the core skill, made many times per round.
- **Red snap arrow** points from your wrong gap to the correct one — immediate,
  *located* feedback on exactly where your ordering was off.
- **Correct placement reveals the "when"** — the timestamp is the reward, so
  reading dates is the win.
- **The log ribbon** fills continuously as epochs scroll off the rail, making the
  deep-time squeeze visible over the whole round, not just at one lock.
- **The catalog spans cosmology and Earth history**, so mastery means ordering
  the compressed early universe *and* the rapid-fire recent billions of years.

## Honesty
All timestamps are standard cosmology/geology approximations and are monotonic in
`rank`. No date is invented to fill a gap; if an epoch is added, its `when` and
`tSec` must be consistent and sourced, per the accuracy gate in AGENT.md.
