# EDUCATION.md — Cosmic Timeline

> The E in GAMES. The education is IN the mechanic: you cannot win without learning the chronological order
> of the universe and the real moment each epoch happened.

## Learning objective
By playing, the student internalizes:
1. **The sequence of cosmic history** — the named epochs from the Big Bang to now, in order.
2. **The real "when"** — the timestamp each epoch reveals when placed correctly (3 minutes vs 380,000 years
   vs 13.8 billion years).
3. **Deep-time intuition** — why a logarithmic timeline is needed: the universe's first second holds as much
   structure as the billions of years since. Early epochs spread out; recent eons cram together.
4. **Causal ordering** — you can't have atoms before nuclei, stars before atoms, galaxies before stars, or a
   Sun before galaxies. Order is causation.

## The epochs (chronological)
| # | Epoch | When (after Big Bang) | What happens |
|---|-------|------------------------|--------------|
| 1 | Big Bang | t = 0 | Space, time, energy and the expansion begin from a hot dense state. |
| 2 | Inflation | 10⁻³² s | The universe doubles in size dozens of times in a flash, smoothing it. |
| 3 | Quark Soup | 10⁻⁶ s | Too hot for protons — a plasma of free quarks and gluons. |
| 4 | First Nuclei | 3 min | Protons + neutrons fuse into the first hydrogen and helium nuclei. |
| 5 | CMB / Recombination | 380,000 yrs | Atoms form; light breaks free as the cosmic microwave background. |
| 6 | Dark Ages | ~50 Myr | No stars yet — only cooling hydrogen gas in the dark. |
| 7 | First Stars | ~200 Myr | Gravity ignites the first stars, flooding the cosmos with light. |
| 8 | First Galaxies | ~1 Gyr | Stars gather by the billions into the first galaxies. |
| 9 | Our Sun Forms | ~9.2 Gyr | A cloud collapses into the Sun and its planets, including Earth. |
| 10 | Life on Earth | ~10 Gyr | The first single-celled life appears in Earth's young oceans. |
| 11 | Now | 13.8 Gyr | You, reading this — 13.8 billion years after it all began. |

## How the mechanic teaches
- **Wrong-order arrow snaps red** at the exact link that's out of sequence — immediate, located feedback.
- **Correct placement reveals the "when"** — the timestamp is the reward, so reading dates is the win.
- **The log ribbon** plots each locked epoch by `log10(time-since-Big-Bang)`, making the squeeze visible.
- **Acceleration adds finer early epochs** (Inflation, Quark Soup, Nucleosynthesis), so mastery means
  resolving the most compressed, least-intuitive part of cosmic history.

## Honesty
All timestamps are standard cosmology approximations. No date is invented to fill a gap; if an epoch is
added, its `when` and `tSec` must be consistent and sourced, per the accuracy gate in AGENT.md.
