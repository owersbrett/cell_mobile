# POTATUHS.md — Life Cycle v2

> How this game carries the brand. The organism scale's headliner is the potato itself.

## The through-line
A life cycle is the cleanest possible statement of the Potatuhs premise: **the tuber IS the next
seed.** A potato is an organ of the plant, but plant an eyed potato and it sprouts a whole new
organism — the cycle closes from tuber back to seed potato. Walking the Potato lap in this game
(seed potato → sprout → plant → flower → tuber → seed potato) makes "what counts as an organism" a
thing the player does with their thumb, not a thing they read.

## Voice fit
- **Butter (CMO):** *"It comes back around. Watch."* — brief, certain, and quietly obsessed with
  where the loop STARTS. The wheel literally returns to its origin; that's the part Butter can't
  stop looking at.
- **Russ (CEO):** *"uhhh... so the potato is the seed? And the plant? At the same time? ...yeah."* —
  the cycle-as-identity puzzle is exactly his kind of idea.

## On-brand by construction
- The potato is a first-class playable organism, not a cameo — it sits in the tier-1 rotation with a
  full 5-stage wheel and the headliner fact.
- Colour-coding follows the Potatuhs dark palette via `metaColor` (orange / sienna / air-force /
  life-green), so each metamorphosis type reads as a brand hue.
- The juice (orbs, glow, particles, gold streak fire) comes from the shared `fx.dart` toolkit — the
  same premium-render language every Potatuhs mini-game speaks.

## Why it earns its place in the cell
The organism scale asks "where does one life begin and end?" — and the answer the brand likes is "it
doesn't; it loops." This game is that answer as a mechanic. It is also the GAMES-rubric exemplar for
the UX pass: a flat quiz turned into a spatial, accelerating, self-teaching cycle-walk without losing
a single biology fact.
