# Bubbles v2 — Education (E)

**Concept:** Eternal inflation and the bubble (pocket) multiverse.

## The idea
In **eternal inflation**, space is filled with a high-energy **false vacuum**
that expands exponentially and never stops expanding everywhere at once. Here and
there, quantum tunnelling lets a patch drop to a lower-energy **true vacuum**: a
**bubble universe** nucleates and grows at near light-speed. Each bubble is a
self-contained "pocket universe," potentially with its own physical constants.

The decisive twist: **the false vacuum between bubbles inflates faster than the
bubbles can fill it.** New space is created between bubbles more quickly than
bubbles expand into it, so the sea is **never** consumed — inflation is
*eternal*, and it spawns an unbounded number of bubble universes. This is one of
the leading routes to a physical **multiverse**.

## Education IN the mechanic
The game is a tuned analogy, not a lecture:

| Physics | In Bubbles v2 |
|---|---|
| False vacuum inflating forever | The field + expanding inflation rings; you can never fill it |
| **Quantum nucleation is spontaneous** | The vacuum nucleates bubbles itself — you don't place them (v2 makes this exact: tunnelling is not chosen) |
| A bubble growing at light-speed | Each bubble inflating its radius |
| Bubbles must stay causally separate | Overlap = COLLIDE and SPOIL |
| A bubble collision is predicted + telegraphed | The red WARNING ARC before two touch |
| A matured pocket universe | A gold, harvestable bubble |
| Inflation outruns the bubbles | The ramp + final cascade flood faster than you can harvest |

**The v2 sharpening:** in the original you *tapped the void to nucleate*, which
implied you create universes. Physically you don't — quantum tunnelling is
spontaneous. v2 makes nucleation entirely the vacuum's, so the player is an
**observer/harvester** of pocket universes, not their author. The lesson is the
same and the analogy is tighter.

**The lesson is felt:** no matter how fast you harvest, the sea keeps churning
out more bubbles faster than you can tend them. That impossibility *is* eternal
inflation — the score caps not because you failed, but because the multiverse
can't be filled.

## Talking points (for sessions / quizzes)
- Why "eternal"? Expansion outpaces bubble formation, forever.
- Each bubble = a pocket universe; constants can differ between them.
- You don't nucleate universes — the false vacuum does, spontaneously, by
  quantum tunnelling.
- Bubble collisions are a real prediction — cosmologists hunt for their imprint
  in the cosmic microwave background.
