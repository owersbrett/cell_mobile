# Superposition — POTATUHS.md

**Scale:** multiverseAll — the widest lens in the catalog. Here the cell-biology journey
opens all the way out to the quantum substrate every potato is ultimately made of.

## Brand fit
The multiverse scale is where Hot Potato Games gets to be weird and cosmic, and quantum
mechanics is the strangest physics there is — a perfect home for the **"uhhh..."** beat.
Russ would stare at the oscillating wavefunction, mutter *"uhhh... is it up or down?"*, and
the game's whole answer is: **both, until you look.** That tension — measuring a potato that
refuses to be one thing — is the joke and the lesson at once.
