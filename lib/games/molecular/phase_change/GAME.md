# Phase Change — GAME (Manual)

**Scale:** molecular · **Verb:** control-to-target · **Score unit:** states

## Premise
You are the heat source for a beaker of matter. Tap-and-hold **HEAT** or **COOL**
to drive a substance's energy up or down and land it on the **target state of
matter** — SOLID, LIQUID or GAS — then hold it there.

## Rules
- Read the prompt at the top: `MAKE IT: SOLID / LIQUID / GAS`.
- Hold **HEAT** to add energy, **COOL** to remove it. Heat also bleeds away on
  its own — keep tapping to maintain a hot state.
- The molecules show the truth: a **locked lattice** (solid), **flowing**
  jiggle (liquid), or **flying apart** (gas).
- Hold the matter in the **target state** until the green bar at the top fills.
  That scores one state and snaps you to a **new substance** with a new target.

## The catch — latent heat
At the melting and boiling points the temperature **stalls on a plateau**: the
energy you add goes into breaking bonds (the phase change), not into raising the
temperature. While on a plateau the matter is "in transition" and counts as **no
state** — you have to keep pushing energy *through* the flat stretch to cross a
boundary. The live curve at the bottom (temperature vs. energy-in) draws both
plateaus so you can see exactly where they are.

## Scoring
- **+points** each time you reach and hold the target state.
- Points scale with **level** (every success raises it) and your **streak**.
- **Streak** counts clean holds — *overshooting* past the target to the far
  extreme breaks it back to 1.

## How to win
Hold the most target states before time runs out. (`durationSeconds` 52.)

## Accelerates
- Heat **bleeds away faster** every level — harder to hold a hot state.
- The **hold time** required shrinks slightly each level.
- The **substance changes** every success: water, wax, mercury, glass, iron —
  each with its own melting / boiling points and plateau widths.
