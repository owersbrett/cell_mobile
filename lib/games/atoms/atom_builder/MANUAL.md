# Atom Builder — How to Play

> Player-facing. Anyone reading this should understand the game.
> This is one entry in the Manual and a source file for the deployable Manual app.

**Scale:** Atoms · **Type:** Solo (chase the high score) · **Length:** timed session (~45–60 s)

---

## The goal

Particles are streaming across the screen. Tap the right ones at the right time to build a real
atom — growing it shell by shell from a single hydrogen proton all the way to xenon. The further
you grow your atom before the clock runs out, the higher your score.

---

## How to play

- **Three kinds of particles float across the field:**
  - Red circles with `+` are **protons** — these set your element. More protons = heavier element.
  - Grey circles with `n` are **neutrons** — these stabilize your nucleus.
  - Blue circles with `−` are **electrons** — these fill the shells around your nucleus.

- **Tap a particle to collect it** into your atom. The atom lives in the lower half of the screen.
  You don't have to tap precisely — any tap near a particle will grab it.

- **Build toward the next noble gas** shown in the top panel. The current target starts at Helium
  (2 of each particle) and climbs: Neon → Argon → Krypton → Xenon. You can't unlock the next
  shell until the current one is perfectly balanced.

- **Keep the nucleus balanced.** If you collect protons faster than neutrons, the nucleus becomes
  unstable — it glows amber and shakes. Leave it unbalanced too long and a proton decays away.
  Always grab neutrons alongside protons.

- **Electrons need a nucleus first.** You can't add an electron if you already have as many
  electrons as protons. Grab a proton first, then its electron.

- **Watch the element name update.** As your proton count rises, the panel at the top shows which
  element you're currently building — Hydrogen, Carbon, Oxygen, Nitrogen, and so on.

---

## How you score

- **+5** for collecting any particle your atom needs.
- **−10** for collecting a particle your atom doesn't need (shell already full).
- **−3** for tapping an electron when there's no proton for it to orbit.
- **+50** each time you complete a noble-gas shell (Helium, Neon, Argon, Krypton, Xenon).
- **+25 bonus** when you hit Nitrogen (7 protons), Phosphorus (15), Sulfur (16), or Potassium
  (19) — the four nutrients every potato needs to grow. A banner fires and the N-P-S-K indicator
  in the corner lights up.

---

## Tips

- Protons and neutrons are roughly equally important — don't focus only on protons or the nucleus
  will destabilize and decay.
- As your streak builds the field speeds up. If it gets overwhelming, focus on one type at a time
  and let the others fly past — a wrong grab costs more than a miss.
- The fertilizer bonuses (N, P, S, K) are free points on the way to every noble-gas checkpoint.
  You'll pass through all four just by playing normally.

## Did you know? (the WOW part)

Every element you build was **forged somewhere specific in the universe** before it ended up in
your potato:

- **Hydrogen** formed 380,000 years after the Big Bang — it's still the most abundant element in
  the universe and the fuel that powers the sun.
- **Carbon** (6 protons) was forged in the cores of dying red giant stars by fusing three helium
  nuclei together. It's the backbone of every starch molecule in a potato.
- **Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Sulfur, Potassium** — the four fertilizer elements the game rewards —
  were forged in stellar cores and scattered by supernova explosions. They drifted through space,
  landed in soil, and eventually grew the potato on your plate.

The atom you're assembling on screen has a history 13.8 billion years long.

---

## What it teaches

How real atoms are built — that atomic number is just a proton count, that electrons orbit in
fixed shells, that nuclear stability needs neutrons to balance protons — and that the elements
your potato is made of were forged in stars.
