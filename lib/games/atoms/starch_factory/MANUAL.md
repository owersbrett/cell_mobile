# Starch Factory — How to Play

> Player-facing. Anyone reading this should understand the game. This is one entry in the
> Manual (and a source file for the deployable Manual app). Plain language, no code.

**Scale:** Atoms · **Type:** Solo (survive as long as possible) · **Length:** until starch depletes (~30–90+ s)

---

## The goal

You are inside the potato's amyloplast — the organelle that turns glucose into starch. Coloured
glucose molecules are rolling in on a conveyor belt. Route each one into the correct funnel before
it gets there on its own, or the chain breaks and your starch reserve drops. Keep the starch bar
above zero as long as you can.

---

## How to play

- **Three lanes run across the screen.** Each lane has a hexagonal funnel (endpoint) on the right
  coloured to accept a specific type of molecule.

- **Molecules scroll right automatically.** You don't control their speed.

- **Tap a lane to select it** (a border appears around it). Then **tap a different lane** to swap
  all molecules between the two lanes — they slide smoothly to their new path.

- **Tap the same lane again to deselect** without swapping.

- **The funnels swap positions** on a timer. A white ring pulses around them ~1.5 s before the
  swap so you have warning. After the swap, molecules that were heading to the right funnel may
  now be heading to the wrong one — quickly swap them back.

---

## How you score

- **+10 points** (base) for each molecule routed to the correct funnel.
- **Combo bonus:** three or more correct routings in a row multiplies your points. Keep routing
  correctly and the multiplier keeps climbing.
- **Emergency bonus:** an extra +10 when you route correctly while the starch bar is critically low.
- **Wrong routing:** no points and the starch bar takes a hit.
- **Starch bar = your life.** It slowly drains over time AND drops on wrong routings. Correct
  routings refill it a little. Reach zero — game over.

---

## Tips

- Watch the funnel colours, not the molecule colours — after a swap, your mental model of
  "which lane takes what" has flipped. Re-read the funnels every swap.
- The swap warning pulse is your cue to plan ahead. Before the swap fires, think about which
  molecules are mid-flight and which lane they'll need after.
- A combo multiplier is worth protecting. One wrong routing kills the streak — if a routing is
  risky, it may be better to let the molecule reach the wrong funnel than to panic-swap and
  mislabel more molecules behind it.
- Later in the game a fourth and fifth lane unlock. More lanes = more swap options but also more
  to track. Slow down, breathe, and focus on the leader (the molecule closest to the endpoint).

---

## What it teaches

Inside every potato, an organelle called the amyloplast does exactly this: it takes glucose
(a six-carbon sugar) and links the carbons into long chains — starch. Carbon is the backbone of
every chain. Route glucose, build starch, feed the potato.
