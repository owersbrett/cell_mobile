# Skin Layers — EDUCATION.md

The education lives **inside the mechanic**: to score you must place each skin
layer at its correct depth, and tapping any tile reveals what that layer holds
and does. You cannot win without learning the order and the roles.

## Learning objectives
1. **The three major layers, in depth order:** Epidermis (surface) → Dermis
   (middle) → Hypodermis (deep fat).
2. **The epidermis is itself layered** (surface → deep): Corneum → Granulosum →
   Spinosum → Basale.
3. **The dermis holds the working structures** — glands, hair roots, vessels and
   nerves — between the epidermis above and the fat below.
4. **Depth order is functional, not arbitrary:** dead waterproof cells form the
   outer shield; living dividing cells sit just beneath; fat insulates deepest.

## The layers (surface → deep) and what each does

### Epidermis (the barrier)
- **Stratum Corneum** — dead, flattened cells form a tough waterproof shield.
- **Stratum Granulosum** — cells fill with keratin and begin to die.
- **Stratum Spinosum** — spiny cells lock together for strength.
- **Stratum Basale** — new skin cells divide here; holds the pigment cells
  (melanocytes).

### Dermis (the working layer)
- **Papillary dermis** — loose tissue; capillaries feed the epidermis above.
- **Sebaceous gland** — oils the hair and skin to keep it supple.
- **Hair follicle** — the root pocket that grows each hair.
- **Nerve ending** — senses touch, pressure, pain and temperature.
- **Reticular dermis** — dense collagen gives skin its strength and stretch.
- **Sweat gland** — coiled gland that cools you by releasing sweat.
- **Blood vessel** — feeds the skin and helps regulate body heat.

### Hypodermis (the cushion)
- **Hypodermis (subcutaneous fat)** — insulates, cushions, and stores energy.

## Why "comes alive"?
When the stack is correct, the skin can do its job: the barrier seals, vessels
feed it, glands work, nerves sense, fat cushions. A correctly ordered slice is a
*functioning organ* — the celebration dramatizes that the structure is what
makes the function possible.

## Misconceptions the game corrects
- "Skin is a single sheet." — It's a depth-ordered **stack** of distinct layers.
- "The epidermis is one thing." — It has four named strata.
- "Glands/hair/nerves are separate from skin." — They live **within** the dermis.
