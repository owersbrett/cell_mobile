# Twitch — EDUCATION.md (the **E** in GAMES)

**Scale:** Tissue (skeletal muscle) · learning is *in the mechanic*, not a quiz bolted on.

## The big idea
Muscles contract when a **nerve signal** (an action potential) reaches the
**neuromuscular junction** and triggers the contractile machinery. The game's core
loop — wait for the signal, fire on the beat — is that cause-and-effect made playable.

## Concept 1 — Excitation: the nerve drives the muscle
A motor neuron fires; the signal travels down the axon to the junction; the muscle
responds. In Twitch the travelling pulse is the action potential and the strike zone
is the junction. A muscle does not contract on its own — it waits for the signal,
which is why **timing**, not mashing, is the skill.

## Concept 2 — The sliding-filament model
A sarcomere is the contractile unit, bounded by **Z-discs**. Thin **actin** filaments
anchor to the Z-discs; thick **myosin** filaments sit in the center. On contraction,
myosin cross-bridges pull the actin inward so the filaments **slide past each other**
and the sarcomere **shortens** — the filaments themselves don't shrink, they overlap
more. The game draws exactly this: a clean hit slides the Z-discs inward over the
fixed myosin, and the muscle visibly shortens.

## Concept 3 — Twitch, summation, and tetanus
- A single stimulus produces one brief **twitch** that then relaxes.
- If a new stimulus arrives *before* the muscle fully relaxes, the contractions
  **summate** (add together) — force is higher than a lone twitch.
- Stimulate rapidly enough and the twitches fuse into a smooth, sustained maximal
  contraction: **tetanus**.

In Twitch this is the relaxation rate vs. signal cadence: one isolated on-time tap
relaxes before the next signal, but fast, accurate taps stack contraction past the
**TETANUS** line and hold it there for bonus force. Faster cadence at higher levels
is exactly why real rapid firing produces fused tetanus.

## Takeaway
Nerve signal → junction → sliding filaments → shortening; and rate of stimulation
sets whether you get a single twitch or fused tetanus. Players feel the timing
relationship rather than memorizing it.
