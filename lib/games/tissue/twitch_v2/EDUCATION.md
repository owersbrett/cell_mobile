# Twitch v2 — EDUCATION.md (the **E** in GAMES)

**Scale:** Tissue (skeletal muscle) · learning is *in the mechanic*, not a quiz.

## The big idea
Muscles contract when a **nerve signal** (an action potential) reaches the
**neuromuscular junction** and triggers the contractile machinery. The core loop
— wait for the signal, fire on the beat — is that cause-and-effect made playable.

## Concept 1 — Excitation: the nerve drives the muscle
A motor neuron fires; the signal travels down the axon to the junction; the
muscle responds. The travelling pulse is the action potential and the strike zone
is the junction. A muscle never contracts on its own — it waits for the signal,
which is why **timing**, not mashing, is the skill.

## Concept 2 — The sliding-filament model
A sarcomere is the contractile unit, bounded by **Z-discs**. Thin **actin**
anchors to the Z-discs; thick **myosin** sits in the center. On contraction,
myosin cross-bridges pull actin inward so the filaments **slide past each other**
and the sarcomere **shortens** — the filaments don't shrink, they overlap more.
A clean hit slides the Z-discs inward over the fixed myosin and the muscle
visibly shortens.

## Concept 3 — Twitch, summation, and tetanus
- One stimulus → one brief **twitch** that then relaxes.
- A new stimulus arriving *before* full relaxation makes contractions
  **summate** — force exceeds a lone twitch.
- Stimulate rapidly enough and twitches fuse into smooth, sustained maximal
  contraction: **tetanus**. In-game this is the relaxation rate vs. firing rate —
  fast, accurate taps stack contraction past the **TETANUS** line and hold it.

## Concept 4 — Muscle fatigue (new in v2)
A real muscle cannot hold maximal tetanus indefinitely — sustained firing
depletes it and **force declines**: fatigue. v2 makes this the central skill: the
**fatigue strip** fills while you hold tetanus, the bonus force decays toward
zero, and the muscle must **rest to recover** before it can fuse hard again. This
both teaches a genuine physiological limit and removes the unfair "hold forever
for infinite points" exploit of v1.

## Takeaway
Nerve signal → junction → sliding filaments → shortening; firing **rate** sets
whether you get a single twitch or fused tetanus; and sustained tetanus
**fatigues**, so real strength is rhythmic, not held. Players feel these
relationships rather than memorizing them.
