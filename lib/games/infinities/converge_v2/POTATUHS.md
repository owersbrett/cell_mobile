## Scale & cast
**Infinities** — the outermost BioScale, past the universe: pure mathematics. It
holds *Tangent* (derivatives) and *Area Under* (integrals); Converge is the
**distinct** third — **limits and infinite series**. v2 keeps that identity and
lowers the calculus floor so it reads at a party, not only in a calculus class.

## Why v2 (the UX pass)
Converge is one of the *phenomenal five* — best lesson + standout chart. The one
named gap was a **high calculus floor**: telling that the harmonic series
diverges while it crawls is not a <3s party call. v2 keeps the soul (the full
series roster, both modes, the streaming partial-sum chart) and adds a **fading
coach cue** + an **honest trend readout** so a newcomer makes a reasoned call,
not a guess — then the scaffolding withdraws as difficulty climbs.

## Brand voice
Russ squints at the harmonic series: *"uhhh… it gets smaller every step, how does
that run off to infinity?"* — that "uhhh…" is the whole game, the honest pause
before the limit lands. v2 hands Russ a hint just long enough to get going, then
takes the training wheels off. Butter: *"Add up infinitely many potatoes and
somehow you still get a finite pile. We've always converged. Don't worry about it."*
