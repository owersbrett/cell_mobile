# EDUCATION.md — Nothings (cell)

> The educational blocks that occupy the Nothings scale, and which game(s) engage each.
> Source data: `lib/data/scales/nothings_entities.dart` (5 blocks).
> Nothings is the **abstract realm** — math, word, sound, waves, light. Not material yet.

- **Scale (cell):** nothings
- **Games on this scale:** `big_bang` (1 of up to ~5 slots filled). Designated **first game**
  of the whole Explore the Cell experience.

## Lore: the three building blocks of Nothings
This scale runs on **light**, **darkness**, and **matter** (see the Big Bang GAME.md):
- **Light** = "on" = 1 — the good; gathers into **stars**.
- **Darkness** = "off" = 0 — the medium everything takes place in.
- **Matter / dark matter** — the hazard; it "shouldn't exist yet" (matter belongs to the next,
  material scale).

## Association verdict
**KEEP** Big Bang as the first game — the light/darkness/matter lore now engages **4 of 5 blocks
strongly** (Binary Nothing, The Void, Nothing as Something, Emergence) and touches **Zero**. A
future math/word game can deepen **Zero** and **Binary Nothing** further. Nothings is solidly KEPT,
with ~4 open slots (math, word, sound/wave, deeper light) for the abstract realm.

## Potato angle
The empty field before anything is planted (the Void / darkness); 0/1 as "no seed / seed."
The deeper hook: **light → stars → (later) the forged elements** that build a potato. From light
in the dark, the long road to potato-stuff begins. Larger scales pay this off (Earth-as-a-potato-farm).
