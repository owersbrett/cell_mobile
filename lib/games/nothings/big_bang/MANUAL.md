# Big Bang — How to Play

> Player-facing. This is the first game in Explore the Cell — your introduction to the whole
> experience.

**Scale:** Nothings · **Type:** Solo (chase the high score) · **Length:** ~timed round

## The goal
Everything begins in **darkness**. Tiny points of **light** drift through it. Gather enough light
and you can **ignite stars**. But **matter** is starting to appear where it shouldn't yet — keep
it out of your grasp.

## How to play
- **Tap anywhere** to open a **void** — a little well that **pulls in nearby light** on its own.
- Drink in enough light and a **star ignites** in the sky.
- **Darkness** is just the space you play in — you don't collect it.
- **Avoid pulling in matter** (the heavy, wrong stuff). It shouldn't exist yet, and sucking it in
  sets you back.

## How you score
- Every mote of light your void drinks = points.
- Igniting a star = a bonus and a new star on the field.
- Swallowing matter = a penalty.
- Make the most stars you can before time runs out.

## Tips
- Place your void where the **light** is thickest and the **matter** isn't.
- It's better to wait for a clean cluster of light than to grab everything.

## What it teaches
The dawn of the universe: at first there is only **light** and **darkness** (on and off, 1 and 0).
Light gathers into the **first stars** — and only later does **matter** appear. From light in the
dark, everything (eventually, a potato) begins.
