# Digest v2 — Potatuhs fit

## Brand surface
- Dark **ink** tract on the deep ink background; the orange→sienna→gold brand
  energy reads through the digestive palette (stomach sienna, gold "ready" halo
  = the brand `gold`). Uses `GameFx.atmosphere`, `GameFx.orb`, `FxBurst`, `FxPop`
  and the Outfit body font — the shared Potatuhs mini-game kit, no bespoke style.
- On-brand wink: the first food morsel is a **potato** (`_kFoodColors[0]`).
  Potatuhs runs the tract; the tater goes first.

## Where it sits
- Vertical: **hotpotatogames**, the Summer "GAMES" cycle — a GAMES-rubric title
  (G game / A AGENT.md / M GAME.md / E EDUCATION.md / S session-reentry via the
  host) in the `cell_mobile` launch artifact (explore-the-cell.web.app).
- Scale: `organSystem` in the Explore-the-Cell ladder of bio scales.
- Ships **alongside** the original `digest` per the UX Refinement Pass
  "coexist, then judge" policy — both A/B-comparable; the judge picks the keeper.

## Voice
Plain, earnest, never winks at the camera (Butter's register). The tract is the
tract; the lesson is the lesson. The only flourish is the potato going in first.
