# Digest v2 — Education (E)

## Learning objective
Players learn the **sequence of the human digestive tract** and the **specific
job of each organ** — and *where in the tract the body actually absorbs
nutrients and water*.

## The lesson is the mechanic
In the original `digest`, the organ→action mapping was only *labeled*: a button
sat under each organ and any press advanced the lit food, so you could clear the
tract without ever knowing that the stomach churns or the small intestine
absorbs. The teaching was decorative.

In v2 you **cannot** progress without supplying the correct action for the
organ the food is currently in. Pressing CHURN on food in the mouth, or WATER on
food in the small intestine, stalls it and breaks your combo. So every point
scored is a correct organ→function recall — the assessment *is* the play.

## What it teaches, concretely
- **Sequence:** MOUTH → ESOPHAGUS → STOMACH → SMALL INTESTINE → LARGE INTESTINE.
  The verb bar is drawn in this order, reinforcing the path each meal takes.
- **Per-organ function:**
  - Mouth — mechanical breakdown (**chewing**) and the start of digestion.
  - Esophagus — **swallowing** / peristalsis pushes food down (no absorption).
  - Stomach — **churning** with acid and enzymes into chyme.
  - Small intestine — **the main site of nutrient absorption** into the blood
    (scored highest to mark where the body actually gains nutrients).
  - Large intestine — **water reabsorption** before waste is expelled.
- **A systems idea:** digestion is a *pipeline* — one bolus per organ, and the
  front must clear before the next can move. Backups teach throughput, not just
  the parts list.

## Scaffolding → mastery
A first-timer matches the icon on the food's organ to the icon on a verb button
(easy to learn). Mastery is recalling all five organ→action pairs from memory,
fast, with five morsels ready at once (hard to master). The difficulty curve
carries the learner from recognition to recall.

## Accuracy notes
Simplified for a 55-second arcade round: real absorption is continuous and the
esophagus/mouth do small chemical digestion too. The headline facts — the
ordered path and that the small intestine is the primary absorption site with
the large intestine reclaiming water — are correct and are the takeaways.
