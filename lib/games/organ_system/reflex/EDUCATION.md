# EDUCATION.md — Reflex (organSystem)

> The educational payload of the Reflex game, and how it is delivered *in the mechanic* rather than
> as a text screen. Subject: the **spinal reflex arc** — the nervous system's fastest circuit.

- **Scale (cell):** organSystem
- **Game:** `reflex` — strong, literal edu tie (the play surface IS the diagram being taught).

## The concept — the reflex arc
A reflex is an involuntary, near-instant response to a stimulus, routed through the spinal cord
**without waiting for the brain**. The five stages, in order, are exactly the five nodes on screen:

| Stage | On-screen node | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Stimulus** | Receptor (orange flash) | A change is detected — a tap on the patellar tendon, a hot stove. |
| **Sensory neuron (afferent)** | Receptor → Spinal cord (blue limb) | Carries the signal *toward* the CNS. |
| **CNS / integration** | Spinal cord (green hub) | An interneuron relays straight to the motor neuron — the decision is made here. |
| **Motor neuron (efferent)** | Spinal cord → Muscle (gold limb) | Carries the command *away* to the effector. |
| **Response** | Muscle (contracts/flashes) | The effector acts — the leg kicks, the hand withdraws. |

## Why it bypasses the brain (the load-bearing lesson)
The game draws the **brain faded, above the cord, on a dotted "bypassed" link.** That is the whole
point: a reflex arc resolves at the spinal cord, so the signal travels a far shorter loop than it
would to the brain and back. Fewer synapses + less distance = milliseconds saved — which, for a hot
stove or a fall, is the difference between safety and a burn. The **damage ring** dramatises this:
react before it fills, or the slow path costs you.

## How the teaching is embedded (not bolted on)
- **You can't score without tracing the real circuit.** Every successful tap plays the impulse along
  afferent → CNS → efferent → effector, in order, every trial — repetition burns the sequence in.
- **The decoy teaches discrimination.** A false cue flashes at the *brain*, not the receptor;
  reacting to it is penalised. Reinforces that the reflex is **receptor-driven**, bottom-up.
- **The false-start penalty teaches that a reflex is reactive, not anticipatory.** You cannot
  pre-fire; you must genuinely *respond* to the stimulus.
- **Reaction-time readout (ms)** makes the abstract "reflexes are fast" concrete and personal.

## Real-world anchors
- **Patellar (knee-jerk) reflex** — the classic monosynaptic stretch reflex a doctor tests.
- **Withdrawal reflex** — yanking a hand off a hot surface before you consciously feel pain.

## Potato angle
Light — this is a human-body story. Optional nod: plants have no nerves, so a potato's "responses"
(tropisms, turgor changes) are chemical and slow — the contrast underlines what nervous-system speed
buys an animal. Keep the focus on the arc.
