# EDUCATION.md — Digest

> The educational component of the **Digest** game (the **E** in GAMES). The learning is delivered
> *through the mechanic* — you can't play without rehearsing the biology.

## Learning objectives
A player who finishes a few rounds can:
1. **Recall the order of the digestive tract:** MOUTH → ESOPHAGUS → STOMACH → SMALL INTESTINE →
   LARGE INTESTINE. The game forces left-to-right routing in exactly this sequence.
2. **Match each organ to its job** (the action it asks for): mouth **chews**, esophagus **swallows**
   (peristalsis), stomach **churns** (acid + muscle → chyme), small intestine **absorbs nutrients**,
   large intestine **absorbs water**.
3. **Name where absorption happens** — the highest-scoring actions are nutrient absorption in the
   **small intestine** and water absorption in the **large intestine**, the two facts students most
   often confuse. The score gradient teaches it: those stages are worth the most.
4. **Understand digestion as a pipeline** — each stage must finish (ripen) and the next must be clear
   before food moves on, mirroring how the tract processes a meal in stages, not all at once.

## How the mechanic teaches (no separate quiz)
- **Sequence:** food only ever advances forward, one stage at a time — repetition burns in the order.
- **Function:** the action button names the organ's job (CHEW / SWALLOW / CHURN / NUTRIENTS / WATER);
  doing the right action *is* the answer.
- **Timing (ripening):** the sweep ring shows that chewing/churning take time — you can't shortcut a
  stage, which models real processing.
- **Absorption emphasis:** scoring weight + the "+NUTRIENTS" / "+WATER" popups foreground the two
  absorption sites.
- **Misconception correction:** pressing the wrong/early action gives immediate `TOO SOON` /
  `NOTHING HERE` feedback and costs the streak — errors are corrected in the moment.

## Facts surfaced in play
- The **small intestine** is where ~90% of nutrient absorption occurs.
- The **large intestine** mainly reabsorbs **water** (and salts) from the remaining material.
- The **esophagus** uses **peristalsis** (muscular waves) to move the bolus — there is no absorption
  there; its only job is transport, which the game models as a pure "advance" with no nutrient payoff.
- A chewed, churned mass becomes **chyme** before entering the small intestine.

## Standards alignment (informal)
Middle-school life science: human body systems — structure and function of the digestive system;
sequence of organs; sites of mechanical vs. chemical digestion and absorption.
