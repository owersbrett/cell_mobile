# Circulate — GAME.md (the Manual)

**Scale:** organ system · **Verb:** DELIVER / ROUTE · **Id:** `circulate`

## Premise
You are the body's circulation. The heart holds a reserve of bright-red
**oxygenated** blood. Push it out along the **arteries** to the organs that
need oxygen, then send the spent **deoxygenated** blue blood back to the
**lungs** to recharge. Keep every organ perfused while demand climbs.

## How to play
- **Tap an organ** to route oxygenated (red) blood to it. Its O₂ ring refills
  and you score. Each delivery spends part of the heart's reserve — the blood
  left at the heart darkens toward blue (deoxygenated).
- **Tap the lungs** to route the blue blood back for recharge. The reserve
  reloads with O₂ and the heart reddens again.
- Organs constantly burn O₂. When one drops below ~⅓ it flashes **LOW O₂**.
  Refill it before it hits zero and **STARVES**.

## Scoring
- **+6** base per delivery, **up to +24** for rescuing a near-empty organ
  (the lower it was, the more it's worth — prioritise the reds).
- **Streak:** consecutive deliveries with no organ flatlining. A starve resets
  the streak. 20+ = mastery on the results screen.
- Score unit: **deliveries**.

## How to win
Most O₂ deliveries when time runs out wins. Let an organ starve and you lose
the streak — keep the whole body perfused.

## The two loops (this IS the lesson)
- **Systemic loop:** heart → arteries → organs (red, oxygen out).
- **Pulmonary loop:** organs → veins → heart → lungs (blue, recharge).
Running the reserve dry forces a recharge — you cannot deliver without it,
exactly as the body cannot deliver O₂ without returning blood to the lungs.

## Acceleration
More organs join over the round (3 → 6) and per-organ O₂ demand climbs ~1.9×,
so deliveries must come faster and recharges must be timed, not reactive.

## Duration
50 seconds. Host owns the clock, countdown, score HUD and results.
