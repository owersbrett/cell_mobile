# Circulate — POTATUHS.md

How this game serves the brand and the GAMES rubric.

## GAMES completeness
- **G — Game:** `circulate_game.dart` — playable single-painter routing game.
- **A — Agent:** `AGENT.md` (this folder) owns the game.
- **M — Manual:** `GAME.md` declares the rules.
- **E — Education:** `EDUCATION.md` — circulatory-system lesson, in-mechanic.
- **S — Session:** rising-edge reset; a round closes and a fresh one starts
  clean via the host.

## Vertical fit (hotpotatogames · cell_mobile)
Part of the organ-system scale in Explore The Cell — the body-wide DELIVERY
counterpart to the cell-scale and tissue-scale games. Teaches circulation by
making the player BE the circulation, not read about it.

## Voice
Calm, earnest, never winking (Butter's register). The body just works; you
keep it working. "Red goes out, blue comes back. Don't worry about it."

## Distinctness
Deliberately NOT "Heartbeat" (single-beat heart-chamber game). Circulate is
whole-body oxygen logistics across the systemic and pulmonary loops.
