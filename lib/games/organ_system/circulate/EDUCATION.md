# Circulate — EDUCATION.md

## Learning objective
Understand the human circulatory system as a **double loop** that delivers
oxygen to the body and recharges it at the lungs — and feel why the two loops
depend on each other.

## Concepts taught (each is a game mechanic, not a text panel)

| Concept | In the game |
|---|---|
| **Oxygenated vs deoxygenated blood** | Red reserve = oxygen-rich; spending it darkens the heart toward blue (oxygen-poor). |
| **Systemic circulation** | Heart → **arteries** → organs. Routing red blood out is delivering O₂ to tissues. |
| **Pulmonary circulation** | Organs → **veins** → heart → **lungs**. Tapping the lungs sends blue blood to reload with O₂. |
| **The heart as a pump** | The heart orb beats continuously and pushes each routed pulse outward. |
| **Tissue O₂ demand** | Organs constantly burn oxygen; busy bodies demand more (demand ramps), so delivery must keep pace. |
| **Why you must return blood to the lungs** | The reserve gate: you literally cannot deliver O₂ once the reserve is dry until you recharge — modelling that oxygen is only reloaded at the lungs. |
| **Perfusion / hypoxia** | An organ starved to zero O₂ "STARVES" — a stand-in for tissue hypoxia when perfusion fails. |

## The organs (systemic targets)
Brain, Liver, Muscle, Kidney, Stomach, Limbs — real high-demand consumers of
oxygenated blood. The brain and working muscle drain fastest, mirroring their
real metabolic rates.

## Talking points for a session host
- "Red goes out, blue comes back." The colour shift on the heart is the whole
  oxygenated/deoxygenated story in one gauge.
- "Why can't I just keep delivering?" Because oxygen is only picked up at the
  lungs — the recharge step is pulmonary circulation made playable.
- The flashing organ is hypoxic tissue calling for perfusion; rescuing the
  lowest-O₂ organ scores the most, rewarding triage.

## Stretch questions
- Which loop has lower oxygen — pulmonary arteries or systemic arteries?
  (Pulmonary arteries carry deoxygenated blood — the one place arteries are
  "blue". The game's blue heart→lungs pulse is that exception.)
- Why does demand rising feel like exercise? (Active tissue consumes O₂ faster,
  so the heart rate and delivery rate must climb.)
