# Collider — How to Play

> Player-facing.

**Scale:** Particles · **Type:** Solo (chase the high score) · **Length:** ~45 seconds

## The goal
Two glowing particles race around a ring in opposite directions. **Tap the exact moment they
cross** to smash them together. Land clean hits to climb to faster rings and score more.

## How to play
- Watch the two particles close in on each other.
- **Tap anywhere** right as they meet — dead-on is a **PERFECT**, just-off is **CLOSE**.
- Tapping when they're far apart is a miss.
- Each good hit bumps you to a **faster ring**; two misses in a row drops you back.
- **Shake your phone** to speed the particles up — riskier, but the rhythm is yours to master.

## How you score
- PERFECT scores most; CLOSE scores some; misses cost you.
- Higher rings are faster and worth more.

## Did you know? (the fun part)
Every **PERFECT** collision uncovers a real particle — *in the order scientists actually found
them*, from the **electron (1897)** all the way to the **Higgs boson (2012)** — with who found it
and when. It doesn't change your score; it's just there to discover.

## What it teaches
How particle physics is really done — smashing beams together to reveal what matter is made of —
and the century-long story of every particle we've found.
