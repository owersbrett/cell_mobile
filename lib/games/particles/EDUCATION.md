## The WOW layer — discovery timeline
Both particle games surface the **real, chronological discovery timeline** (see
`PARTICLE_TIMELINE.md`): each PERFECT collision reveals the next particle in history —
`name · year · who`. It's **non-scoring auxiliary flare** that makes "what all the particles are"
the takeaway of the scale. The 4 blocks above are a subset of the full 17-entry timeline.
