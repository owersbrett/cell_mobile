# Accelerator — How to Play

> Player-facing.

**Scale:** Particles · **Type:** Solo (chase the high score) · **Length:** ~45 seconds

## The goal
Pump energy into the beam and hold it steady in the **green band**. Hold it long enough and the
beam **collides** — scoring points and uncovering a new particle.

## How to play
- **Tap** to pump energy up the gauge — it drains on its own, so keep tapping.
- Hold the needle **inside the green band** (not too low, not too high).
- Keep it there for a couple of seconds to **trigger a collision** and score.
- Each level the **band gets narrower** and the energy drains faster — settle into the rhythm.

## How you score
- You earn points every moment you're in the band, plus a big bonus each collision.
- Higher levels are harder but worth much more.

## Did you know? (the fun part)
Every collision uncovers a real particle, **in the order scientists found them** — electron (1897)
through the Higgs boson (2012) — with who found it and when. Just there to discover; it never
changes your score.

## What it teaches
How a real accelerator works — ramping particles to exactly the right energy before colliding them
— and the timeline of every particle humanity has discovered.
