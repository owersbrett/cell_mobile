# The Plant Envelope — How to Play

> Player-facing. Anyone reading this should understand the game.
> This is one entry in the Manual and a source file for the deployable Manual app.

**Scale:** Organelle · **Type:** Solo (chase the high score) · **Length:** timed session (~45–60 s)

---

## The goal

You're looking at a cross-section of a plant cell boundary. Molecules are trying to get in and
out. Tap to let the right ones through and block the wrong ones — keep the vacuole pressurized,
pack the amyloplast with starch, and stop solanine from forming before time runs out.

---

## How to play

- **The screen is a layered cell wall.** From outside in: the Cell Wall (outer), the Plasma
  Membrane (the real gatekeeper), then the cell interior with the Central Vacuole, a Chloroplast,
  and a pale oval labeled **Amyloplast — Starch Vault.**

- **Molecules drift toward the interior. Tap them to gate them** when they reach the Plasma
  Membrane band:
  - **Blue drops (Water)** — tap to admit. Water fills the vacuole and keeps the cell firm.
  - **Orange hexagons (Glucose)** — tap to admit. Glucose feeds the starch vault.
  - **Grey doubles (CO₂)** — tap to admit. CO₂ keeps the chloroplast busy and stops solanine.
  - **Red spikes (Toxin)** — tap to block. Do not let these in.

- **The Turgor gauge on the left** shows how full the vacuole is. If it drops too low, the cell
  goes limp and you lose points each second. Too high — tap a dotted **plasmodesmata channel** in
  the cell wall to vent the overflow to a neighbor cell.

- **The Amyloplast fill gauge** shows starch granules stacking up inside the Starch Vault. Every
  5 glucose admitted earns a +15 bonus. This is the potato payoff — you're packing the starch
  that ends up in a potato chip.

- **Watch for sunlight bursts.** When a bright wedge sweeps across the wall, if CO₂ hasn't been
  admitted recently, the chloroplast goes idle and a yellow solanine wave spawns. Tap the wave
  at the Chloroplast band before it reaches the vacuole or you lose 15 points and get a
  "GREEN POTATO" warning.

---

## How you score

- **+8** for each correct gate (admitting what should come in, blocking what should stay out).
- **−10** for a wrong gate (blocking water, admitting a toxin).
- **+10** for blocking a solanine wave at the chloroplast.
- **−15** if a solanine wave reaches the vacuole.
- **+15** every time the amyloplast fills 5 more starch units.
- **−5 per second** while turgor pressure is too low.
- **+5** for venting overflow through a plasmodesmata channel.

---

## Tips

- CO₂ has a dual role: it feeds the chloroplast AND prevents solanine. Keep admitting it.
- The amyloplast fill bonus is your best scoring loop — prioritize glucose.
- Solanine windows are tight (2 seconds). When the sunlight burst fires, shift attention to the
  chloroplast band immediately.

---

## What it teaches

A plant cell isn't just one membrane — it's a system of nested boundaries, each with a job.
The plasma membrane decides what gets in. The cell wall provides the rigid outer armor. The
central vacuole uses water pressure to keep the whole structure firm. The amyloplast packs away
sugar as starch. And when sunlight hits an unworked chloroplast, the cell produces solanine as a
defense — which is exactly why green potatoes are mildly toxic. Every mechanic in this game is a
real thing the cell is doing right now.
