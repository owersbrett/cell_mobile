# Powerhouse & Skeleton — How to Play

> Player-facing. Anyone reading this should understand the game.
> This is one entry in the Manual and a source file for the deployable Manual app.

**Scale:** Organelle · **Type:** Solo (chase the high score) · **Length:** timed session (~45–60 s)

---

## The goal

Two things are happening at once. On the left: feed glucose into the mitochondrion to mint ATP
and keep the cell powered. On the right: snap tubulin segments onto the microtubule lattice to
keep the cytoskeleton intact. Miss either track and the cell suffers. Master both at once and earn
a "Powered Skeleton" combo bonus.

---

## How to play

### Left track — The Powerhouse

- **Orange hexagons drifting downward are glucose** (`C₆H₁₂O₆`). Tap one to feed it into the
  mitochondrion at the bottom-left.

- **The mitochondrion takes 1.5 seconds to process each glucose**, then ejects 2 ATP tokens into
  the pool at the top of the screen.

- **Watch the ATP pool.** Every few seconds, a cellular demand fires — a label pops up saying
  "RIBOSOME NEEDS ATP" or "SPINDLE NEEDS ATP" and drains one token. If the pool is empty when
  that happens: −10 points and a CELL STALLED flash.

- **Keep the pool between 2 and 8 tokens.** Below 2 is risky. Above 8, the excess ATP degrades
  and scores nothing — don't over-produce.

### Right track — The Skeleton

- **Teal rectangles drifting across the right side are tubulin segments.** Tap one to snap it
  onto the nearest incomplete microtubule arm anchored to the centrioles.

- **Complete an arm (5 segments) to score +20.** After it glows, the arm resets and you start
  growing it again — microtubules are always rebuilding in real cells.

### Center — Peroxisome intercept

- **Every so often a jagged white spark streaks across the middle.** That is a free radical —
  a byproduct of all that ATP production. Tap it before it reaches the far side and the peroxisome
  absorbs it (+8 points). Miss it and it damages the nearest microtubule arm.

### Combo

- **Powered Skeleton:** complete a microtubule arm when the ATP pool is at 5 or higher and both
  the power and the structure of the cell align — +30 bonus.

---

## How you score

- **+5** for each glucose fed.
- **+3** for each ATP token minted (×2 per glucose = +6).
- **+5** when a cellular demand fires and the pool has enough ATP.
- **−10** when a demand fires and the pool is empty.
- **+5** per tubulin segment snapped.
- **+20** for completing a 5-segment microtubule arm.
- **+30** for the Powered Skeleton combo.
- **+8** for intercepting a free-radical spark.
- **−2 arm segments** if a spark hits the lattice.

---

## Tips

- A rhythm of one glucose every 2 seconds keeps the ATP pool comfortably in the sweet spot.
- Watch the arm progress — tap a segment right after feeding glucose so neither track falls
  behind.
- The free radicals come on a timer. After you see one, mentally prepare for the next in ~10–14
  seconds; don't let it surprise you.

---

## What it teaches

Mitochondria really do turn glucose into ATP — that's not a metaphor. The cell really does run on
ATP as its energy currency, and every cellular job from building proteins to pumping water across
a membrane costs ATP tokens from the pool. The microtubule lattice really is a dynamic scaffold
that grows and shrinks. And peroxisomes really do catch the free radicals that energy production
generates. This game is cellular respiration and cytoskeletal dynamics at tap speed.
