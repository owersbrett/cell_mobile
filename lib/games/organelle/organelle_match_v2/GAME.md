# Organelle Match v2 — Manual (the M in GAMES)

**Scale:** organelle · **Duration:** 60s · **Score unit:** points
**Spec id:** `organelle_match_v2` · **Tagline:** "Know the worker AND the job — both ways."

## The pitch
A rapid organelle⇄function quiz that never stalls. A prompt appears; you tap one
of four answers. It confirms instantly and the next prompt is already loading —
the round visibly accelerates into a final OVERDRIVE.

## How to play
- **MATCH (forward):** a clue names a job ("…burns sugar with oxygen to make
  energy") — tap the **organelle** that does it.
- **RECALL (reverse):** an organelle is named ("What is the job of the
  Mitochondria?") — tap the **job** that matches it.
- Questions are mixed; reverse RECALL shows up more in the back half.

## Scoring (rubber-banded, capped — fair in pass-and-play)
- **Base points rise as the round goes on** — a late answer is worth more than
  an early one, so a slow starter can still catch up.
- **Speed bonus:** the faster you answer inside the (tightening) window, the
  bigger the bonus.
- **Streak multiplier:** every 3 correct in a row adds +1×, **capped at ×3** —
  nobody runs away.
- **A miss costs nothing** — it just resets your streak. Read the inline fact,
  push on.

## The climax — OVERDRIVE
In the final 12 seconds the field turns gold, the speed window collapses, and
**all points double**. The speed bonus goes double-or-nothing: a fast answer
pays 2×, a slow one earns no bonus. The lead is never safe until time runs out.

## How to win
Most points when time runs out wins.

## Rules (registry copy)
- Match a job to its organelle — or an organelle to its job. Tap one of four.
- Faster answers score more; later answers are worth more (catch-up).
- 3 correct in a row = +1× multiplier, capped at ×3.
- Final 12s is OVERDRIVE: points double, speed bonus is double-or-nothing.

## Education (the E lives in the mechanic)
The full organelle→function map for all 9 organelles (nucleus, mitochondria,
ribosome, chloroplast, Golgi apparatus, lysosome, endoplasmic reticulum,
vacuole, cell membrane). Mixing forward and reverse forces you to know each
mapping **both directions**, not just pattern-match a clue string. Misses
surface the correct organelle, its job tag, and a fun fact. See EDUCATION.md.
