# Organelle Match v2 — Education (the E in GAMES)

## Learning objective
Map each of the 9 core organelles to its function — **both directions**. The
player should be able to (a) name the organelle that performs a stated job, and
(b) state the job of a named organelle. Bidirectional recall is the depth axis
that pure forward matching lacks.

## The content (organelle → function)
| Organelle | Job | One fun fact |
|---|---|---|
| Nucleus | Control center — holds the DNA | DNA sits behind a double membrane dotted with pores. |
| Mitochondria | Powerhouse — makes ATP | Carries its own DNA — once a free-living bacterium. |
| Ribosome | Builds proteins | A cell can run millions of them at once. |
| Chloroplast | Photosynthesis — makes sugar | Green because chlorophyll soaks up red and blue light. |
| Golgi apparatus | Packaging & shipping | A post office: addresses each protein for delivery. |
| Lysosome | Digestion & recycling | Sacs of enzymes that recycle worn-out parts. |
| Endoplasmic reticulum | Synthesis & transport | Rough ER makes protein; smooth ER makes fats. |
| Vacuole | Storage | A plant's central vacuole can fill most of the cell. |
| Cell membrane | Boundary & gatekeeper | A fat bilayer — some molecules slip through, others can't. |

## How the mechanic teaches
- **Forward (MATCH):** a function clue → pick the organelle. Tests retrieval
  from job to name.
- **Reverse (RECALL):** an organelle → pick the job. Tests retrieval the other
  way, which catches players who only memorised clue strings.
- **Confusable distractors ramp:** as the round heats up, wrong answers are
  pulled from each organelle's near-miss set (e.g. Mitochondria vs Chloroplast,
  Lysosome vs Vacuole) — the classic pairs students mix up, so the difficulty
  sharpens the exact discriminations that matter.
- **Misses teach, not punish:** a wrong tap costs no points and surfaces the
  correct organelle, its job tag, and a fun fact inline (non-blocking).

## Standards alignment (middle-school life science)
Cell structure & function: students identify organelles and explain how each
contributes to the cell's survival (NGSS MS-LS1-2). The reverse mode pushes past
recognition toward genuine recall.

## Assessment signal
Score reflects speed + accuracy + sustained streaks across both directions.
A high score under the reverse-heavy back half indicates true bidirectional
mastery, not clue-string pattern matching.
