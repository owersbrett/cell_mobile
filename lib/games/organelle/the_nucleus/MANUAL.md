# The Nucleus — How to Play

> Player-facing. Anyone reading this should understand the game.
> This is one entry in the Manual and a source file for the deployable Manual app.

**Scale:** Organelle · **Type:** Solo (chase the high score) · **Length:** timed session (~45–60 s)

---

## The goal

Nucleotides are drifting across the nucleus. Tap the right one to complete the next base pair on
the DNA strand. Build ten pairs in a row to trigger a Transcription Flash — the strand unzips,
mRNA is written, and a ribosome is born. Score as many runs as you can before time runs out.

---

## How to play

- **Four types of nucleotides float across the screen:**
  - Red hexagons = **Adenine (A)**
  - Amber hexagons = **Thymine (T)**
  - Blue hexagons = **Guanine (G)**
  - Green hexagons = **Cytosine (C)**

- **The template strand lives at the bottom.** It shows the next five bases you need to pair.
  The active slot pulses to show which base you're currently filling.

- **Tap the correct matching nucleotide.** DNA pairing has two rules:
  - **A always pairs with T** (and T with A).
  - **G always pairs with C** (and C with G).
  So if the template shows A, tap a T. If it shows G, tap a C.

- **Wrong tap = −8 points** and your combo chain resets.

- **Complete ten correct pairs** and the game enters the **Transcription Flash:**
  - The double helix splits apart.
  - An mRNA strand is written across the gap.
  - The mRNA exits through a nuclear pore.
  - The nucleolus flashes and fires a ribosome out through the pore.
  - You earn a +15 bonus, then a new run begins.

- **Keep your chain alive.** Each clean run without a wrong tap increases your multiplier: ×1 →
  ×2 → ×3 → up to ×4. One wrong tap drops it back to ×1.

---

## How you score

- **+5** (× your chain multiplier) for every correct base pair.
- **−8** for a wrong tap (chain resets to ×1).
- **+20** flat bonus for completing a 10-pair run.
- **+15** flat bonus for each Transcription Flash.

---

## Tips

- Prioritize keeping your chain alive over speed — ×4 turns every correct tap into +20.
- The template strand is your guide. Glance at it before you tap. The active slot is the only
  one you need to fill right now.
- As the clock winds down, nucleotides speed up. If the field gets busy, it's okay to let wrong
  ones drift past — a miss costs nothing. Only a wrong *tap* costs you.

---

## What it teaches

DNA is written in a four-letter alphabet — A, T, G, C — where each letter only ever pairs with
one partner. The Transcription Flash shows exactly what happens when a cell needs a protein: the
helix unzips, an mRNA copy is written, and the mRNA leaves the nucleus to find a ribosome. Every
one of those steps is a real molecular event, playing out at hand speed.
