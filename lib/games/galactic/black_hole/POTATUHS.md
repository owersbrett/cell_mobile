# POTATUHS — Black-Hole Heart

> The POTATUHS lens applied to one game: every game is itself a **Project** with
> Objectives, Tasks, Automations, Testing, UX, Heuristics, and Systems. One
> profile per game (template instance).

- **P — Project:** Black-Hole Heart — the galactic-scale central-engine orbit
  game. Game id `black_hole` on `BioScale.galactic`; a self-contained title built
  to the GAMES rubric, deliberately differentiated from the gentle planet-orbit
  games by its lethal supermassive black hole (Sgr A\*).

- **O — Objectives:** keep a population of stars alive on the knife-edge around the
  event horizon, as long and as tightly as possible. Sub-goal: ride the closest
  survivable orbit, because income and revolution bonuses both scale with
  closeness — while the same closeness decays fastest.

- **T — Tasks (the play to-do list):** read the field · pick a fling that the
  preview labels ORBIT (not SWALLOWED, not ESCAPES) · birth the star · bank its
  revolutions · re-fling decaying orbits before they cross the horizon ·
  repopulate freed slots as the cap grows · recover every orbit after each
  gravitational kick.

- **T — Testing (experimental / in-flight):** first-pass `humanMax ≈ 6000` and
  star thresholds — flagged for retune after real playtest; directional (tidal)
  kicks and a live `v vs v_circular` aim readout are noted but unbuilt.

- **A — Automations (firing in the background):** inverse-square + pseudo-
  relativistic pull integrated in 6 sub-steps · accretion drag that decays every
  orbit (3× inside the ISCO) · continuous closeness-scaled income banked as integer
  deltas · per-revolution bonuses + streak · the live preview running the exact
  flight math to verdict the shot · the growing horizon · the periodic
  gravitational-kick schedule.

- **U — UX:** a pure-black event horizon with a flaring photon ring and lensing
  halo · a tilted, rotating violet→gold→orange accretion disk · a dashed rotating
  ISCO danger ring · stars that glow hotter/whiter the faster they orbit
  (Doppler-bright) with glowing trails · a verdict-colored trajectory preview +
  aim arrow · star-population pips and a "fed N" swallowed counter.

- **H — Heuristics (how you actually win):** the closest survivable orbit pays
  best — but leave margin for the next kick · graze the ISCO, never the horizon ·
  a fling that the preview can't make ORBIT is a wasted star · keep the field full
  (the cap grows, so does your income ceiling) · after a kick, triage: save the
  ones nearest the horizon first.

- **S — Systems (what makes the world feel alive):** one overwhelming mass that
  bends every path · a disk that boils and pulses · a horizon that flares each time
  it feeds · orbits that whip and precess up close · periodic kicks that shake the
  whole field · the Sgr A\* fantasy — the silent four-million-Sun heart the galaxy
  turns around, "we've always been here, we always will be."
