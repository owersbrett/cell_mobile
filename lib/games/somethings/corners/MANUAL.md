# Corners — How to Play

> Player-facing.

**Scale:** Somethings · **Type:** Solo (chase the high score) · **Length:** ~timed round

## The goal
Shapes drift and spin past you. Tap each one **exactly as many times as it has corners** — no
more, no less.

## How to play
- A triangle has 3 corners, a square 4, a pentagon 5, a hexagon 6 — **tap that many times**.
- Your **first tap claims** the shape so it sticks around long enough to finish; keep tapping up
  to its corner count, then **stop**.
- Partway through, **3D solids** start spinning in — now count their **corners (vertices)**: a
  cube has 8, others have more. The shape's name appears when it resolves.

## How you score
- **Exact** corner count = the most points.
- Close still scores, just less.
- Leaving a shape untouched costs nothing — only commit when you're sure.

## Tips
- Watch the spin a beat before you commit — corners are easier to count when you catch the rhythm.
- The big solids (icosahedron 12, dodecahedron 20) are worth the most — but easy to over-tap.

## What it teaches
The birth of **form**: a corner is where two edges meet, and every shape can be "read" by counting
its corners — from flat polygons to the five perfect 3D solids.
