# EDUCATION.md — Somethings (cell)

> The educational blocks that occupy the Somethings scale, and which game(s) engage each.
> **Decision (2026-06-17):** Somethings is the **Geometry / Form** scale — the first thing with a
> *boundary* (a barrier, a container, a shape). Geometry is fundamental and emanates right after
> the beginning (Nothings). The old philosophical blocks relocate (see below).

- **Scale (cell):** somethings
- **Games on this scale:** `corners` (1 of up to ~5 slots filled).

## Lore: the first form
After Nothings (light/darkness/matter), the first **something** is the first thing with an
**edge** — a boundary that separates inside from outside. That is geometry. Form emerges before
matter settles: point → line → angle → shape → solid. The **boundary/container** idea here is the
seed that later becomes the cell membrane (a shape that holds an inside).

## Association verdict
**KEEP** — Somethings becomes the Geometry/Form scale and Corners is a strong, direct fit
(counts corners of polygons and vertices of the platonic solids). ~4 open slots remain
(e.g., symmetry, tiling/tessellation, the boundary-as-container game that foreshadows the cell).

## Potato angle
The potato is the **anti-platonic solid** — lumpy, irregular, no clean corners. A fun foil to the
perfect forms; counting a potato's "eyes" vs counting a solid's vertices. And the **boundary**
idea (a shape that holds an inside) is the first whisper of the potato's skin / the cell membrane.
