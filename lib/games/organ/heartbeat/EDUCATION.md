# EDUCATION.md — Heartbeat

> The educational component (the E in GAMES). The learning is IN the mechanic: you cannot score
> without routing blood through the heart in the correct order, and you watch it change color as it
> oxygenates.

## The one big idea

**Blood travels through the heart in a fixed loop, and it changes oxygen state on the way.** The game
makes that loop the rule: tap the chambers in order, on the beat, or the flow stalls.

## The circulation loop (what each tap teaches)

```
        BODY  ──(blue, O₂-poor)──▶  RIGHT ATRIUM  ──▶  RIGHT VENTRICLE
          ▲                                                   │
   (red, O₂ delivered)                                  (pumped to)
          │                                                   ▼
   LEFT VENTRICLE  ◀──  LEFT ATRIUM  ◀──(red, O₂-rich)──   LUNGS
```

1. **Body → Right Atrium.** Oxygen-poor (blue) blood returns from the body and enters the right atrium.
2. **Right Atrium → Right Ventricle.** It drops into the right ventricle, the pulmonary pump.
3. **Right Ventricle → Lungs.** Pushed to the lungs. **Here it picks up oxygen and turns red.**
4. **Lungs → Left Atrium.** Oxygen-rich (red) blood returns to the left atrium.
5. **Left Atrium → Left Ventricle.** It falls into the left ventricle, the body's main pump.
6. **Left Ventricle → Body.** Pumped out to the whole body, where oxygen is delivered — and it turns
   blue again, ready to start over. **One full lap = one circulation cycle.**

## Two circulations, one heart

- **Pulmonary circulation** — the *right* heart's job: heart → lungs → heart. Short loop, picks up O₂.
  (In the game: stages RA → RV → Lungs → LA, the blue-into-red half.)
- **Systemic circulation** — the *left* heart's job: heart → body → heart. Long loop, delivers O₂.
  (In the game: stages LA → LV → Body → RA, the red-into-blue half.)

The heart is really two pumps in series. The game's ring shows them as two tinted halves.

## Chambers vs valves

- **Atria** (right + left) are the *receiving* chambers — blood arrives here.
- **Ventricles** (right + left) are the *pumping* chambers — the muscle that pushes blood onward.
- The left ventricle is the strongest because it drives blood to the entire body (systemic circuit).

## Heart rate

Each lap is one heartbeat's worth of pumping. The game's BPM readout rises as your streak grows —
mirroring how a real heart speeds up under demand — and the timing window tightens with it.

## Check-for-understanding (what a player should be able to say after)

- "Blood is blue (low oxygen) on the right side and red (high oxygen) on the left side."
- "It turns red at the lungs and gives up its oxygen at the body."
- "Right heart → lungs is pulmonary; left heart → body is systemic."
- "Atria receive, ventricles pump."
