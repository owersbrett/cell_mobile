# EDUCATION.md — Heartbeat v2

> The educational component (the E in GAMES). The learning is IN the mechanic:
> you cannot score without routing blood through the heart in the correct order,
> watching it change color as it oxygenates — and now, hitting each chamber on
> the correct phase of the cardiac cycle.

## The one big idea
**Blood travels through the heart in a fixed loop, changing oxygen state on the
way — and the heart works in two phases, systole and diastole, that you hear as
"lub-dub."** v2 makes BOTH true in the mechanic: tap in order (the loop) AND on
the right sound (the phase), or the flow stalls.

## The circulation loop (what each tap teaches)
```
        BODY  ──(blue, O₂-poor)──▶  RIGHT ATRIUM  ──▶  RIGHT VENTRICLE
          ▲                                                   │
   (red, O₂ delivered)                                  (pumped to)
          │                                                   ▼
   LEFT VENTRICLE  ◀──  LEFT ATRIUM  ◀──(red, O₂-rich)──   LUNGS
```
1. **Body → Right Atrium.** Oxygen-poor (blue) blood returns and enters the RA.
2. **Right Atrium → Right Ventricle.** Drops into the pulmonary pump.
3. **Right Ventricle → Lungs.** Pushed to the lungs. **Picks up O₂, turns red.**
4. **Lungs → Left Atrium.** Oxygen-rich (red) blood returns to the LA.
5. **Left Atrium → Left Ventricle.** Falls into the body's main pump.
6. **Left Ventricle → Body.** Pumped to the whole body; O₂ delivered; turns blue
   again. **One full lap = one circulation cycle.**

## The new lesson: systole, diastole, and "lub-dub"
The heart is not a continuous squeeze — it alternates two phases, and that
alternation is what you hear through a stethoscope as **lub-dub**:
- **SYSTOLE — "LUB" (S1).** The **ventricles contract**, snapping the AV valves
  shut (the "lub") and ejecting blood to the lungs and body. *In the game: every
  non-atrial stage (Body, RV, Lungs, LV) is tapped on the LUB.*
- **DIASTOLE — "DUB" (S2).** The heart relaxes and the **atria fill and give
  their kick**, while the semilunar valves snap shut (the "dub"). *In the game:
  the two atria (RA, LA) are tapped on the DUB.*

So the required rhythm — **lub·dub … lub … lub·dub … lub** — is an actual
heartbeat: the close LUB-DUB pairs are the chamber hand-offs (Body→RA, Lungs→LA),
and the longer gaps are the diastolic pauses between beats.

## Two circulations, one heart
- **Pulmonary circulation** — the *right* heart: heart → lungs → heart. Picks up
  O₂. (Game: RA → RV → Lungs → LA, the blue-into-red half.)
- **Systemic circulation** — the *left* heart: heart → body → heart. Delivers O₂.
  (Game: LA → LV → Body → RA, the red-into-blue half.)

## Chambers vs the cycle
- **Atria** (right + left) *receive* and fill during diastole — the **DUB**.
- **Ventricles** (right + left) *pump* during systole — the **LUB**. The left
  ventricle is strongest; it drives the whole systemic circuit.

## Heart rate
Each lap is one heartbeat's worth of pumping. BPM rises as your streak grows —
mirroring a real heart speeding up under demand — and the timing window tightens
with it.

## Check-for-understanding (what a player should be able to say after)
- "Blood is blue (low O₂) on the right side, red (high O₂) on the left side; it
  turns red at the lungs and gives up O₂ at the body."
- "Right heart → lungs is pulmonary; left heart → body is systemic."
- "The heart beats in two phases: **systole** (ventricles pump — the *lub*) and
  **diastole** (atria fill — the *dub*)."
- "Atria fill on the dub; ventricles pump on the lub."
