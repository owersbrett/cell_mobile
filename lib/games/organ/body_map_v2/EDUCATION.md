# EDUCATION.md — Body Map v2

> The educational component (the **E** in GAMES). The learning is **in the
> mechanic**: to score, you must place each organ where it actually sits in the
> body. v2 deepens it — beyond *where* an organ lives, the JOB tokens make you
> connect *what it does* to *which organ it is* and *where it sits*.

## Learning objective
By the end of a run a player can:
1. **Locate** the major human organs by region — head, chest, upper abdomen,
   flanks, lower abdomen, pelvis — and roughly left vs. right.
2. **Match function to organ** — "pumps blood → heart", "filters blood into
   urine → kidney", "makes insulin → pancreas", "stores urine → bladder" — the
   added layer that pure position recall leaves open.

## What each placement teaches

| Organ | Where it sits | Its job (JOB token) |
|---|---|---|
| **Brain** | Inside the skull (head) | Thinks & controls the whole body. |
| **Heart** | Centre of the chest, tilted left | Pumps blood; sits between the lungs. |
| **Lungs** | Flanking the heart, both sides of the chest | Breathe in air. |
| **Liver** | Upper-right abdomen, under the ribs | Makes bile; the largest internal organ. |
| **Stomach** | Upper-left abdomen | Digests food from the esophagus. |
| **Spleen** | Upper-left, behind the stomach | Immune defence; filters blood. |
| **Pancreas** | Behind the stomach, centre | Makes insulin and digestive juices. |
| **Kidneys** | Mid-back flanks, one each side of the spine | Filter blood → urine. |
| **Intestines** | Coiled through the lower abdomen | Absorb nutrients. |
| **Bladder** | Low in the pelvis | Stores urine until it leaves the body. |

## How the game reinforces it
- **Ghost ring (levels 1–2):** the correct spot pulses for NAME tokens while you
  learn — scaffolding, preserved from v1.
- **Ghost removed (level 3+):** the scaffold disappears; you must remember.
- **Job tokens (level 2+):** the token names a function, not the organ. You
  recall function → organ → location — a deeper chain than position alone, and it
  pays ×1.5 to reward the harder knowledge.
- **Accuracy bonus:** dropping near the true centre pays a little more — a sharper
  mental map is rewarded, but the region is generous so it never becomes fussy.
- **Landmark-first order:** big organs (brain, heart, lungs) come first; small,
  easily-confused ones (spleen, pancreas, kidneys) join at higher levels once the
  landmarks anchor the layout.
- **Triage under decay:** with several organs waiting at once, you practise the
  whole map at speed rather than one isolated recall at a time.

## Potato tie-in (cross-scale)
The companion **Organ Rush** quiz alternates human organs with plant/potato
organs (root, tuber, stem). Body Map keeps the human body so the spatial map is
unambiguous — but "organs are specialised parts with a place *and a job*" carries
straight across to the potato, and v2's JOB tokens make that place-and-job pairing
explicit.

## Honest scope
Coarse, kid-friendly landmarks, not a surgical atlas. Left/right labels follow the
player's on-screen view (a simplification). JOB strings are deliberately simple,
one-line functions. The aim is a durable mental picture of *where things are* and
*what they do* — exactly the gap a naming-only quiz leaves open.
