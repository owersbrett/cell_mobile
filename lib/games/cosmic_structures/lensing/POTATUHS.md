# POTATUHS — Lensing

> The POTATUHS lens applied to one game: every game is itself a **Project** with
> Objectives, Tasks, Automations, Testing, UX, Heuristics, and Systems. One
> profile per game (template instance).

- **P — Project:** Lensing — the cosmic-structures-scale gravitational-lensing game.
  Game id `lensing` on `BioScale.cosmicStructures`; a self-contained title built to
  the GAMES rubric, deliberately differentiated from the projectile games by having
  **no launch at all** — you bend a continuous light beam with an invisible mass.

- **O — Objectives:** focus a distant galaxy's light onto your telescope by placing
  a dark-matter halo and tuning its mass, then **hold the focus** to take the
  reading — as fast and as tightly as possible. Stretch goal: form a true **Einstein
  ring** by focusing two background galaxies with one halo.

- **T — Tasks (the play to-do list):** read the geometry · drop the halo near the
  light's path · slide the mass until the rays bend home (not under, not over) ·
  nudge position to dead-centre the focus · hold the lock to bank the reading ·
  repeat fast as the detector shrinks, drifts, and a second galaxy joins.

- **T — Testing (experimental / in-flight):** first-pass `humanMax ≈ 4200` and star
  thresholds — flagged for retune after real playtest, along with a `_kG`/mass-range
  check so every generated geometry is reliably solvable; the optional **"spot the
  lensed image"** variant and angled beams are noted but unbuilt.

- **A — Automations (firing in the background):** continuous light emission re-traced
  every frame · per-ray deflection `∝ mass / distance²` with velocity renormalised
  (light = constant speed, pure bending) · the live focus fraction + closest-approach
  precision · the hold-to-lock charge/decay · speed/precision/step/Einstein scoring ·
  the detector drift schedule · the procedural ladder + loop-shrink generator that
  keeps two-source rounds symmetric (hence solvable).

- **U — UX:** an INVISIBLE dark-matter halo shown only by its violet warp — ghost
  core, faint spacetime rings, a mass-brightened dashed boundary · tilted spiral
  background galaxies pouring thin beams · light rays that color-shift **blue → gold**
  the instant they land in the detector · a gold telescope with intake rings, a
  crosshair, a drift arrow, and a filling lock ring · a `FOCUS %` / `LOCKING %` pill,
  a round/loop label, and a one-line hint.

- **H — Heuristics (how you actually win):** bend, don't aim — nudge mass first, then
  walk the halo to centre the bundle · under-bend flies past, over-bend crosses over,
  so hunt the middle · the closer the halo to the beam, the harder it bends (small
  moves, big effect near the rays) · on two-source rounds, park the halo **on the
  detector line** so both beams curve in symmetrically · solve fast — speed is the
  score, and the streak only counts clean, quick reads.

- **S — Systems (what makes the world feel alive):** an unseen mass that warps an
  entire beam of light · galaxies smeared into arcs and rings · the moment two
  sources snap into one ring (the Einstein-ring payoff) · a sky you map by its
  distortions, never by sight — the dark-matter fantasy: an origin you can only
  interrogate by watching the light it bends.
