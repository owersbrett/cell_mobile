# EDUCATION.md — Map the Void (Cosmic Structures)

> The educational payload for `map_void`. The **E** in GAMES. The mechanic *is* the lesson: you spend a
> minute doing exactly what cosmologists do with a galaxy survey — sorting the sky into the three forms
> of cosmic large-scale structure — and the scoring physically rewards the correct intuition that the
> universe is **mostly empty**.

## The big idea: the universe has a shape, and it is a web
Zoom out past galaxies and clusters and the matter of the universe is not spread evenly. It is arranged
in a vast, spongy network called the **cosmic web**. It has three characteristic components:

| Form | What it is | In the game |
|---|---|---|
| **Void** | Enormous, near-empty bubbles — tens to hundreds of millions of light-years across — that hold almost no galaxies. | Patches with 0–few faint strays. The most common region. |
| **Filament** | Thread-like bridges of galaxies and gas strung *between* the dense regions — the "walls and threads" of the web. | A line of galaxies crossing the scope. |
| **Cluster** | Dense knots where filaments intersect — the richest pile-ups, holding hundreds to thousands of galaxies bound by gravity. | A tight blob of packed galaxies. |

Galaxies flow, over cosmic time, *out of* the voids, *along* the filaments, and *into* the clusters at
the nodes. The web is the scaffolding; gravity slowly slides matter toward the densest crossings.

## The striking fact the game teaches: it's mostly void
By volume, the universe is overwhelmingly **void** — depending on how you draw the boundaries, very
roughly **80%+ of cosmic volume is void**, with all the galaxies, clusters and visible structure crammed
into the thin filaments and the nodes between them. That is why the game is **void-heavy on purpose**:
the trap is the human instinct to see structure everywhere and over-tag. The skill the game trains —
holding back, only committing to CLUSTER/FILAMENT when the density truly reads as a knot or a thread — is
the same discipline that makes the lopsided emptiness of the cosmos *felt*, not just stated.

## Why we know this
The web isn't a guess — it's measured. Galaxy redshift surveys (e.g. the CfA "Stick Man," 2dF, and the
Sloan Digital Sky Survey) mapped the 3-D positions of hundreds of thousands of galaxies and revealed the
filaments, walls and voids directly. Cosmological simulations that evolve gravity from the early universe
(seeded by tiny density ripples, shaped by dark matter and dark energy) grow the **same** web — a strong
cross-check between observation and theory.

## Scale anchor (where this sits in the cell ladder)
`cosmicStructures` is the second-largest scale in Explore the Cell: bigger than galaxies, just under
"multiverse / universe-all." Cluster → filament → void is the *texture* of everything at that scale —
the largest pattern that still has a recognizable shape before you run out of universe to zoom through.

## Takeaways a player should leave with
1. Cosmic matter is arranged in a **web**: clusters (knots) linked by filaments (threads) around voids
   (emptiness).
2. The universe is **mostly void** — empty space is the rule, structure is the exception.
3. Galaxies migrate **void → filament → cluster** under gravity over cosmic time.
4. This structure is **observed** (redshift surveys) and **reproduced** (simulations), not assumed.
