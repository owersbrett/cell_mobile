# Hungry Cell — How to Play

> Player-facing. Anyone reading this should understand the game. This is one entry in the
> Manual and a source file for the deployable Manual app. Plain language, no code.

**Scale:** Cell · **Type:** Solo (chase the high score) · **Length:** 45 s

---

## The goal

You are a cell. The world is full of food and danger. Eat everything you can reach. Grow as
large as possible before time runs out. Avoid (or devour) the other cells — depending on how
you compare in size.

---

## How to play

- **Drag anywhere** to steer your cell. It glides smoothly toward where you touch and follows
  your finger as you move it. Release to coast.

- **Eat green pellets** scattered across the map. Each one is worth +2 mass. There are 90 of
  them — they respawn in a new spot after being eaten.

- **Grab glowing organelle pickups.** These are rarer and worth much more (+20 mass). Three
  kinds float around the world:
  - **Mitochondria** (orange pill-shaped) — the powerhouse bonus
  - **Golgi** (purple stacked curves) — the distribution bonus
  - **Ribosome** (cyan cluster of dots) — the builder bonus

- **Eat smaller predator cells.** The red wobbly cells are predators. If your cell is clearly
  bigger than one, you can eat it for +30 mass. A green rim on a predator means it's smaller
  than you — go get it.

- **Flee predators that are larger than you.** If a predator is bigger and touches you, you
  lose −25 mass and get knocked back. After a hit you're invulnerable for 1 second (your cell
  blinks) — use that window to get away.

---

## How you score

Every point of mass you accumulate is your score. The leaderboard at the end shows who grew
the most in 45 seconds.

| Event | Mass |
|---|---|
| Eat a green pellet | +2 |
| Eat an organelle pickup | +20 |
| Eat a smaller predator | +30 |
| Hit by a larger predator | −25 |

---

## Tips

- **Stay near pellets early** — the predators start slow and few. Rack up pellets and pickups
  in the first 20 seconds before the world gets dangerous.
- **Your cell grows visually.** Watch your size compared to the predators. The moment you're
  bigger than one, it's free points — don't let it escape.
- **The predators get smarter.** In the second half of the game, they start chasing you directly.
  Plan escape routes before you need them.
- **Organelle pickups become rarer** as the game progresses. Grab them early when you see them.
- **After a hit, use the 1-second blink window.** You can't be hit again during that window —
  use it to push through a crowd of small predators and eat them.

---

## What it teaches

That in a cellular environment, size is relative power — cells compete for resources, and whether
something is food or a threat depends entirely on how you compare in size.
