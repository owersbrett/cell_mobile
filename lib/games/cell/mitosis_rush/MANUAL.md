# Mitosis Rush — How to Play

> Player-facing. Anyone reading this should understand the game. This is one entry in the
> Manual and a source file for the deployable Manual app. Plain language, no code.

**Scale:** Cell · **Type:** Solo (chase the high score) · **Length:** ~75 s (6 phases)

---

## The goal

One cell needs to become two. You'll guide it through all six phases of mitosis — one quick
challenge per phase. The faster you complete each phase, the higher your score. Too slow and
the cell moves on without you, and you lose the speed bonus.

---

## The six phases (in order)

### 1. Interphase — "TAP the cell to copy DNA!"
The cell is preparing to divide. **Tap inside the glowing cell** over and over — each tap
copies more DNA. You need 20 taps. The cell swells and brightens as it fills up.

### 2. Prophase — "TAP chromatin to condense!"
Loose strands of DNA (the fuzzy blobs) need to be tightened up. **Tap each blob** to condense
it into a compact chromosome. They float around, so keep up! There are 12 to tap.

### 3. Metaphase — "DRAG chromosomes to the plate!"
The chromosomes need to line up in the middle of the cell. A glowing horizontal line marks the
**metaphase plate**. **Drag each chromosome** and drop it near the center line — it will snap
into place. Align all 4 to advance.

### 4. Anaphase — "SWIPE pairs UP or DOWN to poles!"
Each chromosome pair needs to be pulled apart to opposite ends of the cell. Look for the
**↑ and ↓ arrows** on each pair. **Swipe UP or DOWN** on a pair to send it to the right pole.
Do all 4 pairs.

### 5. Telophase — "TAP each nucleus to seal it!"
Two new nuclei are forming. **Tap each nucleus outline** 5 times to seal the nuclear envelope.
Watch the dashed circle fill in as you tap. Seal both nuclei to finish.

### 6. Cytokinesis — "DRAG the furrow to split the cell!"
Almost done! A thin orange line marks the cleavage furrow. **Drag it rightward** to pinch the
cell in two. Keep dragging until the furrow reaches 80% across.

---

## How you score

Each phase gives you **50 base points** for completing it, plus a **speed bonus of up to 100**
based on how much time you had left. A phase completed with lots of time remaining → up to 150
points. A phase where the timer ran out → 50 points.

**Maximum total: 900 points** (150 × 6 phases).

After Cytokinesis you'll see a breakdown of all six phase scores. Gold scores (90+) stand out.
**Tap anywhere to play again.**

---

## Tips

- In **Metaphase**, you don't need to be precise — chromosomes snap to the plate as soon as they
  get close. A quick flick downward is usually enough.
- In **Anaphase**, any vertical swipe counts — you don't need to match the arrow direction
  exactly, just swipe clearly up or down.
- In **Cytokinesis**, drag continuously from left to right without lifting. A slow, steady drag
  across the cell works better than short stabs.
- The **timer bar** at the bottom of the screen turns red in the last 25% of each phase. That's
  your cue to stop being careful and just finish it.

---

## What it teaches

The six real phases every cell goes through to divide — DNA replication, chromosome condensation,
alignment, separation, re-packaging, and the final split into two identical daughter cells.
