# EDUCATION.md — Nutrient Cycle v2

> The educational component (the **E** in GAMES). The lesson is taught **inside the mechanic** — you
> don't read a fact card, you keep a cycle alive and feel the biology do it. The lesson is
> **identical** to the original `nutrient_cycle`; v2 only makes the matter-vs-energy asymmetry the
> thing your hands actually manage.

## Learning objective

After a round, a player can explain:
1. **Matter cycles and is conserved.** A carbon, water or nitrogen atom is never destroyed — it
   moves between **reservoirs** (atmosphere, plant, animal, soil, ocean, cloud, microbes) and comes
   back around. The same atom loops forever.
2. **The transfers are real, named biogeochemical processes** — and they only run in valid
   directions:
   - **Carbon:** photosynthesis · feeding · leaf litter · death · respiration · decomposition.
   - **Water:** evaporation · condensation · precipitation · runoff · transpiration.
   - **Nitrogen:** fixation · assimilation · feeding · death · ammonification · denitrification.
3. **Energy flows ONE WAY and dissipates.** Unlike matter, energy is **not** recycled. It enters the
   living system only at the **sun-driven process** and is lost as **heat** at every subsequent step.
4. **Where energy enters each cycle:** **photosynthesis** captures sunlight into carbon biomass;
   **evaporation** is sun-driven and powers the water cycle; **nitrogen fixation** is intensely
   energy-demanding. These are the system's energy-input steps.
5. **Why ecosystems need constant solar input:** matter can keep cycling on its own, but without a
   steady re-supply of energy the system runs down — the cycle stalls even though all the atoms are
   still there.

## How the mechanic teaches it (no fact cards)

- **You operate the process vocabulary.** The atom's valid moves are the named processes, shown live
  on each option edge. Choosing a move *is* naming the process (photosynthesis, respiration,
  fixation…), and an invalid tap is a **DEAD END** because no real process connects those pools.
- **Matter conserved = the atom is never consumed.** The atom orb (with its element symbol) persists
  the whole round, looping the ring. Closing a loop pays a bonus — matter coming all the way home.
- **Energy one-way = the leak you can see.** Every transfer sheds **heat motes** that drift up and
  vanish and never come back — the atom's glowing **energy aura** dims with each step. You watch
  energy leave the system; you never watch it return except at one place.
- **The sun is the only re-entry.** Energy refills **only** on the gold **☀** edge (photosynthesis /
  evaporation / fixation). To survive you keep routing back to the sun — exactly the biological fact
  that ecosystems depend on a continuous external energy input while their matter merely circulates.
- **The STALL is the definition, felt.** Let energy hit zero and the cycle **STALLS** ("find the
  sun") — the lived consequence of an energy-starved system, even though the matter (the atom) is
  still right there, conserved.

## Misconceptions corrected

- *"Energy cycles like matter does."* No — matter is recycled indefinitely; energy is one-way and
  must be continuously re-supplied by the sun. The game makes you re-fetch it at the producer step.
- *"Nutrients get used up."* No — a nitrogen or carbon atom is conserved and keeps moving between
  reservoirs; what runs out (the stall) is the *energy* driving the transfers.
- *"Any move between reservoirs is possible."* No — transfers only run along real, directional
  processes; a dead-end tap shows there is no process connecting those pools that way.

## Scale fit

Sits on **`BioScale.ecosystem`** alongside Food Web and Predator/Prey. Where those cover *who eats
whom* and population dynamics, Nutrient Cycle covers **biogeochemistry** — how the same matter is
reused across the biosphere while energy streams one-way from sun to heat. It is the cycle-vs-flow
lesson that underpins every food web above it.

## Extensions (future)

- A "second atom in flight" mode for simultaneous routing (the teardown's stretch option).
- Surface real residence-time facts when an atom parks long in a reservoir (carbon in deep ocean vs
  atmosphere).
- A human-impact toggle (fossil-fuel burning shortcuts carbon to the atmosphere; fertiliser floods
  the nitrogen cycle) to show how the loops get unbalanced.
