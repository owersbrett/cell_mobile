# EDUCATION.md — Nutrient Cycle

> The E in GAMES. Nutrient Cycle teaches **biogeochemical cycling** — that matter is *conserved* and
> moves in closed loops while *energy* flows one way — and it does so **in the mechanic**, not in a
> popup. You learn it by routing an atom round and round and feeling the flow meter drain.

---

## The one idea: matter cycles, energy flows

This is one of the deepest rules in all of ecology, and it has two halves:

1. **Matter is conserved and CYCLES.** The atoms that make up living things — carbon, nitrogen, oxygen,
   hydrogen, phosphorus — are not created or destroyed. They move between **reservoirs** (the
   atmosphere, oceans, rocks, soil, and living bodies) in **closed loops**. A carbon atom in your breath
   today may have been in a dinosaur, a limestone cliff, and a potato. The Earth is essentially a closed
   system for matter: what we have is what we recycle.

2. **Energy is NOT conserved the same way — it FLOWS.** Energy enters the biosphere from the Sun, passes
   *one direction* through food chains (plant → herbivore → carnivore), and leaves as low-grade heat. At
   every step most of it dissipates (the second law of thermodynamics). You cannot recycle energy the way
   you recycle matter — which is exactly why the Sun has to keep shining and why **the game's FLOW meter
   only ever drains.** The only way to refill it is to keep *matter* moving.

**In the game:** the atom going round the ring forever = matter cycling (conserved). The flow meter
bleeding away = energy dissipating (not conserved). Two different laws, felt at once.

---

## The three cycles you route

### Carbon cycle
CO₂ in the **atmosphere** is pulled into **plants** by **photosynthesis**, eaten by **animals**,
and returned to the air or soil by **respiration**, **death**, and **decomposition**. Over millions of
years some carbon detours into fossil fuels and rock; burning fossil fuels short-circuits that slow loop
and is why atmospheric CO₂ is rising.

### Water cycle
Water **evaporates** from the **ocean**, **condenses** into **clouds**, falls as **precipitation** on
**land**, and returns to the sea as **runoff** — with a branch where plants release it by
**transpiration**. The Sun powers the whole loop; the total amount of water on Earth barely changes.

### Nitrogen cycle
N₂ gas is ~78% of the air but most life can't use it directly. Bacteria **fix** it into soil nitrates,
plants **assimilate** it into protein, animals get it by **feeding**, and **decomposer microbes** return
it to the soil (**ammonification**) or to the air (**denitrification**). Inventing industrial nitrogen
fixation (the Haber-Bosch process) is what lets us feed billions — and the runoff of excess fertilizer
is what causes ocean dead zones.

## Three things you can watch happen in the game

### 1. The loop closes (conservation)
Route the atom all the way around and it arrives back where it started — **nothing was lost.** That's
the loop bonus. Matter doesn't run out; it returns. This is why "throwing something away" is a fiction —
on Earth there is no "away," only another reservoir.

### 2. The flow drains (energy dissipates)
Stop moving and the FLOW meter empties and the cycle **STALLS.** Cycles don't run themselves — they need
a continuous energy throughput (ultimately the Sun) to keep matter moving. The meter is the second law
of thermodynamics made into a game timer.

### 3. Dead ends (only real processes are valid)
You can't move carbon straight from animal back to plant — there's no process for that. Every legal step
is a **named transfer** (photosynthesis, respiration, fixation, evaporation…). Tapping an impossible step
is a dead end. The constraint *is* the biology: matter only moves where a real mechanism carries it.

---

## Why this matters

- **Climate:** the carbon cycle is the climate. Understanding sources, sinks, and residence times is the
  whole game of decarbonization.
- **Agriculture:** the nitrogen and phosphorus cycles set the limits of how much food the planet grows —
  and their disruption (runoff, dead zones, depleted soils) is a central environmental problem.
- **Ecology generally:** "nutrients cycle, energy flows" is the sentence that organizes an entire
  introductory ecology course. This game is that sentence, playable.

**The takeaway, in one line:** the same atoms get used over and over in closed loops, and it takes a
one-way river of energy to keep pushing them round.
