# Predator & Prey v2 — Potatuhs

## Brand fit
The meadow runs the dark Potatuhs palette: hares in the brand green, lynx in
**Fiery Orange** (`Potatuhs.orange`), hawks in glaucous purple, the climax in the
orange→gold brand energy. Surfaces use `Potatuhs.surface` / ink panels; the dial
fill sweeps red → gold → green using the brand `gold`. Display face
(Bowlby One SC) only for the marquee **PEAK SEASON** callout; Outfit for all UI.

## Why it earns its place
The Hot Potato Games vertical wants party-legible games with real skill ceilings.
v1 had the deepest ceiling in the cohort but was spectator-hostile — a scrolling
ODE graph. v2 keeps the depth and makes the contest **glanceable**: a living
field + a single health dial + a balance-streak number anyone can read across a
room. That's the pass-and-play bar.

## Season hook
Ecology = the farm. A potato pest and its predator are the same coupled boom–bust
one trophic level down — a natural bridge to a CrispyCrisps / PotatoFolk
"keep the field in balance" beat, and an easy explainer reel: *"every predator is
just waiting on its prey — don't crash the thing you're eating."*

## Voice
Butter, on the ecosystem dial: *"Green means the world keeps spinning. Red means
somebody stopped eating. We've always kept the balance. We always will. Don't
worry about it."*
