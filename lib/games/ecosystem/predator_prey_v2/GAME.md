# Predator & Prey v2 — Manual (M)

**Scale:** ecosystem · **Duration:** 55s · **Score unit:** balance (pts) ·
**Win:** Most points when time runs out — earned by keeping BOTH herds alive and near balance.

## The world
A living meadow. Two (then three) populations rise and fall on the real
**Lotka–Volterra** equations:

- **HARES (green)** — prey. Grow on their own toward a **CARRYING CAPACITY (K)**.
- **LYNX (orange)** — predators. Live ONLY by eating hares; with no food they starve.
- **HAWKS (purple)** — an apex predator that arrives past the halfway mark and
  hunts the LYNX. Three populations to juggle.

You don't read a graph. You **watch the herds**: the field fills with green as
hares boom, orange predators swarm in, the green gets hunted away, the lynx
starve, and the hares bounce back. The coupled boom–bust cycle is the lesson,
and you can feel it.

## The standing — the ECOSYSTEM HEALTH dial
The dominant bar at the top is the one thing to watch. It runs **red → gold →
green**:
- **Green** = both populations sit in the healthy band (you're scoring double-time).
- **Gold** = alive but drifting.
- **Red** = a population is collapsing.

The headline number on the right is your **BALANCE streak** — whole seconds held
healthy in a row. That's the mastery metric and the comparable standing for
pass-and-play.

## Controls
- **HARES −/+** — cull or release 12 hares.
- **LYNX −/+** — cull or release 6 lynx.
- **PROTECT A PATCH** — a 3s refuge that halves predation so hares recover
  (7s cooldown).

Every nudge lands with a visible burst + ripple, so you feel it hit the system.

## Scoring (fair, no runaway)
- **+4 / sec** while both species are above the extinction floor.
- **+8 / sec** (doubled in the climax) while both sit in the healthy band.
- **−40** one-off when a species hits zero (it reseeds — never a game-over).

Rates are bounded, so no single lucky swing laps the field; mastery is *time
held in balance*, not a jackpot.

## Pace & climax
The cycle accelerates and shocks (drought / disease / bloom / hawk swarms) come
faster as the round runs. The **final 10s is PEAK SEASON** — balance points
double and the cycle speeds up for a real finish.

## How to win
Steer the coupled system: cull hares before the lynx boom crashes them, release
when they crash too hard, time the refuge through a predator spike. Keep the dial
green as long as you can; longest balance streak + most balance time wins.
